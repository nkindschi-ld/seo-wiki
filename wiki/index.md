- [[meta-keywords-tag]] — The deprecated `<meta name="keywords">` tag: why it's dead for SEO (Google ignores it since 2009, Bing treats it as a possible spam signal), its one real downside (leaks your keyword targeting to competitors in public source), no ranking penalty for keeping it, and no evidence AI crawlers/LLMs read it — recommendation: drop or leave empty.
