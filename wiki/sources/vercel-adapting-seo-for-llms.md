# How We're Adapting SEO for LLMs and AI Search (Vercel, Kevin Corbett & Malte Ubl)

**Citation:** Kevin Corbett, Malte Ubl, "How we're adapting SEO for LLMs
and AI Search," Vercel Blog, 2025-06-10.
https://vercel.com/blog/how-were-adapting-seo-for-llms-and-ai-search

Higher-credibility than most GEO blog content ingested so far: an
engineering-team blog post from a company (Vercel) with first-party
traffic data, not a marketing-agency framework piece. The `34.5%`
AI-Overview click-reduction figure and Tally's ARR figures have no
disclosed independent source/methodology beyond the company's own
reporting — treat those specific numbers as directional.

## Key takeaways

- **First-party case-study data**: ChatGPT drives ~10% of new Vercel
  signups, up from 4.8% and 1% six months prior — a rare first-party,
  named-company data point for AI-referral growth trajectory, distinct
  from this wiki's existing aggregate/third-party traffic-scale
  statistics in [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]].
- **Tally case study, extended with hard numbers**: this wiki already
  has Tally naming ChatGPT its "#1 referral source" (via
  [[sel-what-is-generative-engine-optimization-geo-2026]]); this source
  adds a quantified outcome — $2M to $3M ARR growth in four months,
  attributed to AI search as Tally's primary acquisition channel.
- **"Up to 34.5%" AI Overview click-reduction claim** — no disclosed
  methodology, but directionally consistent with this wiki's more
  rigorously-sourced [[aio-ctr-impact]] finding (cited AIO traffic still
  runs ~38% below a no-AIO baseline) — treat as a soft corroboration,
  not independent confirmation, given the lack of disclosed sourcing.
- **RAG index-sourcing table**, extending [[semrush-chatgpt-search]]'s
  existing ChatGPT-via-Bing finding: ChatGPT, Copilot, **and Meta AI**
  use Bing's index; Google uses its own proprietary index; Perplexity
  uses mixed sources. Meta AI's Bing dependency is new to this wiki.
- **"Frontier concept identification"**: a first-mover framing not
  previously named in this wiki — monitor Twitter/X, Reddit, GitHub,
  Discord for emerging questions competitors haven't covered yet, on the
  claim that "LLMs favor the first or clearest explanation of a concept."
  No disclosed evidence for the claim itself, but it's a reasonable
  extension of this wiki's existing topical-authority/authority-tiering
  guidance.
- **"Could a competitor replicate this tomorrow?" authorship test** — a
  concrete, quotable heuristic for the depth bar behind this wiki's
  existing thought-leadership/primary-research tactics.
- **SSR/SSG/ISR as the concrete remediation** for the "AI crawlers don't
  execute JavaScript" risk already in [[technical-seo-audit-checklist]]
  — a specific web-framework-level fix (not just "avoid JS-dependent
  content") that wasn't previously in this wiki.
- **Expanded organic-citation-seeding channel list** (Reddit, GitHub,
  Hacker News, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Stack Overflow, changelogs, AMAs,
  open-source examples) and a new nuance: **paid links carry reduced
  weight in training data versus organic references** — extends
  existing Reddit/UGC and third-party-authority tactics with a
  paid-vs-organic distinction not previously stated.
- **30/90/180-day content review cadence** — a concrete refresh schedule
  extending this wiki's existing general freshness guidance.
- Measurement guidance (referrer tracking, citation auditing, index
  coverage) substantially restates tactics already in
  [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] and [[sel-integrate-geo-with-seo]].
