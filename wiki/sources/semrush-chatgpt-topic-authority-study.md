# Semrush × Growth Memo — The State of Topic Authority in ChatGPT (2026)

**Citation:** Margarita Loktionova (Semrush), in collaboration with
Kevin Indig / Growth Memo, "The State of Topic Authority in ChatGPT,"
Semrush Blog, 2026-07-20.
[Original](https://www.semrush.com/blog/chatgpt-topic-authority-study/) ·
`raw/articles/semrush-chatgpt-topic-authority-study-2026.md`

One of the largest topic-ownership datasets in the wiki: **1,094 US
categories** tracked monthly in ChatGPT, Jan–Jun 2026, each category a
cluster of **five representative prompts** (definition, comparison,
alternatives, use case, purchase). Spans **220K+ domains, 50K+ brands,
600K+ citations, 220K+ URLs**.

## Key takeaways

- **Most categories are unowned.** Only **15.2%** of categories have a
  clear owner; **31.2%** have an emerging leader; **53.7%** are unsettled.
  Precise thresholds: an *owner* leads mention-share in ≥4 of 5 prompts
  with a ≥5-percentage-point margin; an *emerging leader* tops ≥3
  prompts but misses the owner bar; *unsettled* = no brand in ≥3 prompts.
- **Inverse-demand paradox.** High-search-volume topics are *less* owned
  (11.3% have clear owners) than low-demand ones (19%) — the opposite of
  classic-SEO competition intensity.
- **Traditional SEO metrics barely predict ChatGPT winners.** Comparing
  owners vs. runners-up, owners had higher branded search volume in only
  **55.7%** of pairs, higher organic traffic in **48.4%**, higher
  Authority Score in **52.5%**. Only branded search volume was
  statistically significant, and "the edge was modest." (These are
  directional win-rates, **not** correlation coefficients.)
- **Leadership is sticky once earned.** Clear owners held #1 in **90.4%**
  of month-over-month comparisons. Held leads had a median margin of
  **2.9 points**; flipped leads a median of **1.3 points** — a ~5-point
  margin is roughly the stability threshold.
- **Mentions ≠ citations.** Only **21%** of the most-cited domains in a
  category were also the most-mentioned brand; the two correlate
  *slightly negatively* (**-0.229**). (The related **74%**-of-users-pick-
  the-top-mentioned-brand stat comes from Kevin Indig's *earlier* Growth
  Memo research, not this study.)

## Recommendations

Target topical clusters (not single prompts); measure current topical
authority; concentrate on 2–3 categories where you already have signal;
build authority via broad content coverage + relevant third-party
mentions in ChatGPT's sources + strong entity signals.

## Caveats

- Vendor study (Semrush); ownership/authority framing routes to Semrush's
  AI Visibility Toolkit and Authority Score.
- ChatGPT-only, US-only, six-month window — no cross-engine or
  longer-horizon comparison.
- "Mention share" is the unit throughout; the study measures brand
  *mentions* in answers, distinct from source *citations* (which it also
  tracks separately for the decoupling finding).
