# The AI Mention Effect (Profound)

**Citation:** Laskaris, Nikolas. "The AI Mention Effect." Profound Blog,
2026-07-01. https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/the-ai-mention-effect
(Double-opt-in US privacy panel, 2M+ AI conversations, Jan-Jun 2026,
ChatGPT/Gemini/Google AI Overviews.)

## Key takeaways

- **Platform-specific downstream visit lift**, more granular than the
  wiki's existing Similarweb figure: following a brand mention, users
  visit that brand's site at 1.5-2.5x their forecasted baseline rate
  over 7 days. By platform: Gemini +145% relative (largest relative
  lift), Google AI Overviews +61% (largest absolute lift/volume),
  ChatGPT +48%.
- **Industry variation**: Financial Services/Retail via Gemini (+132%/
  +140%), Software via Google AI Overviews (+128%).
- **Timing distribution** (new to this wiki): 20.5% of first visits
  within 1 hour, 42% within 24 hours; Google AI Overviews is fastest
  (45.7% within 24 hours).
- **Attribution gap, precisely quantified**: only ~2.5% of downstream
  visits carry trackable AI-referral parameters, even after ChatGPT's
  May 2026 "more clickable links" update (+2.00pp lift before →
  +2.16pp after — a modest improvement, not a step change). Over 97%
  of post-mention visits are untagged.
- **Methodology**: a "forecasted backward-placebo" design — comparing
  post-exposure 7-day visit rates against three prior 7-day placebo
  windows per user, rather than a randomized experiment. "AI-exposure"
  is defined narrowly as a brand mention appearing in the AI's
  response but not in the user's own prompt, isolating genuine
  discovery from pre-existing brand interest.
- **Caveats (stated by the authors)**: a site-visit study, not a
  conversion/purchase study; correlational rather than a randomized
  experiment; doesn't fully eliminate selection bias between exposed
  and non-exposed populations.

  previously in the wiki.
