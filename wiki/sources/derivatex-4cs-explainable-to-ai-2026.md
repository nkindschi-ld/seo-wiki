# DerivateX — "The 4 C's of Being Explainable to AI" (Sharma, 2026)

**Citation:** Apoorv Sharma, "The 4 C's of Being Explainable to AI," DerivateX, July
11, 2026. https://derivatex.agency/frameworks/4-cs-of-being-explainable-to-ai/

An **original tactical positioning framework** (+ self-test and attached first-party
research) for becoming *recommendable* by AI engines — the third DerivateX/Sharma
source in the wiki, alongside [[derivatex-two-googles-one-query-aio-vs-serp-2026]] and
[[derivatex-competitor-citation-steal-prompt-2026]]. Written up as the playbook
[[explainable-to-ai-4cs]].

## Key takeaways

- **Legibility beats likeability:** "AI recommends the companies it can explain
  confidently"; winners "are not the best-liked. They are the most legible."
- **The 4 C's:** Category (one plain buyer-searchable bucket), Customer (one narrow
  named segment), Contrast (one differentiator restated without hedging), Consistency
  (identical language across homepage/G2/LinkedIn/Crunchbase/press).
- **Two-minute self-test:** ask 4 engines "what does X do, who's it for, why choose
  it?", score 0–2 per C (/8); 7–8 recommendable, 0–3 invisible.

## Attached research (first-party, small samples)

- **2026 State of AI Visibility benchmark:** 50 B2B SaaS, 1,400 prompts, 4 engines —
  ~half scored <50/100 on AI presence despite near-perfect favorability (corroborates
  [[victorious-q2-2026-quarterly-search-report]]'s recognition-vs-mention gap and
  [[ai-shortlist-effect]]).
- **Authority Inversion study:** 233 ChatGPT recommendations, 40 categories — 84% of
  citations from vendor self-descriptions/niche sites; **G2 and Capterra cited zero
  times**.

## Evidence quality & conflict

- The **4 C's are original positioning guidance**, sound as discipline; company scores
  (Clio 89, ServiceTitan 68 vs. Jobber 41) are illustrative, not validated benchmarks.
- Samples are small and partly ChatGPT-only (n=50 companies; n=233 recommendations).
- **Conflict logged:** the "G2/Capterra zero, 84% vendor self-description" finding
  tensions with the wiki's third-party-authority/review-platform guidance
  ([[geo-content-optimization-tactics]], [[victorious-q2-2026-quarterly-search-report]],
  and DerivateX's own AIO review-site 5% figure). Added as an **unresolved Conflicting
  Evidence** entry in [[ai-citation-landscape]] with a recommendations-vs-citations
  reconciliation; not silently absorbed. See `wiki/log.md` for the conflict entry.
