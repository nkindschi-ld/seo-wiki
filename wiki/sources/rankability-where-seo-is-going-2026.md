# Where SEO Is Going: The 2026 State of AI Search (Rankability)

**Citation:** Rankability. "Where SEO Is Going: The 2026 State of AI
Search — AI, AI Agents, and the Rise of Answer & Generative Engine
Optimization." Data-driven forecast report, 2026-06-15.
rankability.com

## What this source is

A vendor forecast report with **two tiers of evidence**:

1. **Primary, disclosed-methodology data**: a Google Keyword Planner
   export of 3,751 keywords across 48 months (June 2022-May 2026),
   seeded around SEO/AI/AI-agents/AEO/GEO, grouped into themed rolling
   12-month volume trend lines. This is genuinely new to this wiki —
   *search-demand* trends for these terms themselves, not
   citation/visibility-correlation data.
2. **Secondary, undisclosed-inline-sourcing claims**: several
   statistics (AIO prevalence ~48%, the ranking/citation overlap
   figure below) are stated in the narrative without an inline citation
   to which of the report's ten listed secondary sources (HubSpot,
   SEO.com, Marketing LTB, Growth-Engines, SEJ, Ahrefs, Mersel AI,
   commercetools, eMarketer, GrowthSpree) backs which number. The report
   states only that "the direction of every major claim was
   cross-checked against industry research" — a directional check, not
   a sourced figure. Treat tier-2 claims with the same caution as other
   undisclosed-methodology sources already in this wiki (e.g.
   [[singlegrain-google-ai-overviews-ultimate-guide-2025]]).

## Key findings — primary keyword-demand data (Tier 1)

Rolling 12-month search volume by theme (12 months to May, each year):

| Theme | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI (general) | 91.1M | 124.6M | 176.6M | 326.9M |
| SEO | 27.0M | 25.8M | 47.7M | 33.4M |
| AI agents | 38.9K | 145.7K | 634.9K | 843.2K |
| GEO | 0 | 2.8K | 15.6K | 59.8K |
| AEO | 0.4K | 1.0K | 7.4K | 27.3K |

- **AI search demand is ~10x SEO's size and still accelerating**
  (+260%/3.6x since 2022, +85% YoY most recently) — "everything else is
  a ripple on top of this," per the report.
- **SEO interest peaked mid-2025 (~47.7M) and has fallen ~30% since**
  (to ~33.4M by May 2026, ~166K searches/month declining) — the
  **first sustained decline** in the report's 4-year panel. In the
  report's own rebased/indexed-momentum chart, SEO is the only one of
  the five tracked themes trending *below* its June 2024 baseline;
  everything else (AI, agents, AEO, GEO) is above it.
- **AEO and GEO went from statistical noise (2023) to a named
  discipline (2025) to a plateau (early 2026)**: AEO sits ~17% below
  its September 2025 high, GEO ~45% below its August 2025 high. GEO
  carries roughly **2x AEO's raw search volume**, yet the report notes
  practitioners are converging on "AEO" as the standard label anyway —
  its interpretation is that GEO caught on as the catchier public name
  while AEO wins in professional/boardroom use because it maps to a
  measurable outcome (getting cited). The report predicts the two
  terms consolidate toward one within ~18 months. Both remain roughly
  three orders of magnitude smaller than SEO search volume, even at
  their peak.
- **AI agent demand grew ~22x (2022-23 to 2025-26)**, ramped hardest
  through 2024-25, peaked around early 2026, then pulled back ~40% —
  a hype-to-deployment volatility signature, not a collapse. The report
  frames this as agents becoming *consumers* of search results
  themselves, shifting the optimization question from "does my page
  rank?" to "does the agent choose, trust, and transact with my brand?"

## Key claims — secondary/undisclosed sourcing (Tier 2)

- **"~48% of tracked queries now show an AI Overview"** — no inline
  source given. Sits much closer to
  [[singlegrain-google-ai-overviews-ultimate-guide-2025]]'s
  already-flagged-as-an-outlier "50%+" figure than the ~15-16%
  consensus from [[semrush-ai-overviews-study-2025]] and
  [[sel-what-is-generative-engine-optimization-geo-2026]] recorded in
  [[aio-ctr-impact]]'s existing Conflicting Evidence section. Added
  there as a further data point on the higher-estimate side, not as
  independent confirmation (undisclosed sourcing).
- **"Overlap between top-10 Google rankings and AI-answer citations
  collapsed from ~75% (mid-2025) to 17-38% (early 2026)"** — no inline
  source given, and no dataset of the report's own supports a
  citation-overlap measurement (the Keyword Planner panel measures
  search *demand*, not citation composition). This would be a
  significant temporal claim if verified — it describes the same
  ballpark starting point as [[ahrefs-b2b-seo-statistics-2025]]'s
  static "76% of AI Overview citations pull from Google's top-10
  organic" figure, then asserts a sharp decline neither Ahrefs nor
  [[ipullrank-optimize-for-sge]] (93.8% of AIO citations *not* in
  top-10, a different snapshot in time) measured directly. Flagged as
  **unverified, not corroborating or contradicting on its own** — it
  extends an existing unresolved tension in [[ai-citation-landscape]]
  without adding verifiable evidence to resolve it.
- **Organic click decline 38-60% "in field studies"** and **80-83%
  zero-click rate on AIO-present searches (AI Mode ~93%)** — both
  broadly consistent in direction with figures already established in
  [[aio-ctr-impact]] (citation-status CTR data) and
  [[semrush-ai-overviews-study-2025]] (zero-click-rate data, though
  that source found the zero-click rate *decreasing* 33.75%→31.53%
  over 2025 — a different metric/direction than this report's static
  80-83% figure; see that page's existing note on why these aren't
  necessarily contradictory). Not independently adding new information
  since the underlying field studies (SEJ, Ahrefs) are already cited
  elsewhere in this wiki via other sources.

## Practical framing (the report's own recommendations)

The report's playbook (reframe the offer as "AI search visibility,"
track "share of model"/citations across ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity/
Copilot/Google AI, optimize for being quoted, defend high-intent
transactional pages, prepare structured data for agents, own
first-party audience channels) restates tactics and framing already
well-established elsewhere in this wiki
([[geo-content-optimization-tactics]], [[optimizing-for-the-agentic-web]])
rather than adding new tactical guidance.
