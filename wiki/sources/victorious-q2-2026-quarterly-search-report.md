# Victorious Q2 2026 Quarterly Search Report — Recognition vs. Mentions

**Citation:** Michael Transon (Founder & CEO, Victorious), "Victorious Q2
2026 Quarterly Search Report" (AI brand recognition vs. mention study),
published on Search Engine Journal, 2026-07-29.
[SEJ writeup](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-brand-mention-study-victorious-spa/582765/) ·
`raw/reports/victorious-q2-2026-quarterly-search-report.md`

Tests **175 brands** across five verticals (legal, healthcare, SaaS,
financial services, ecommerce) on **8 AI platforms** (ChatGPT, Claude,
Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Meta
AI), analyzing **49,391 citations**. Separates a *recognition* test (can
the model describe the brand?) from a *mention* test (does the brand
appear in answers to buying-stage prompts?).

## Key takeaways

- **The recognition–mention gap.** **96%** of brands were described
  accurately, yet **89%** never appeared in AI answers to category-
  research questions. Being known is near-universal; being recommended
  is rare — a clean, large-sample statement of the "known but not picked"
  phenomenon.
- **Platform recognition varies at the tail.** Most engines exceeded
  **83%** recognition accuracy across verticals, but Perplexity
  recognized **<55%** of SaaS/ecommerce brands and Meta AI only **46%**
  of SaaS brands.
- **Mentions correlate with off-site footprint, not owned content.**
  Referring domains **0.49** and third-party web mentions **0.45** were
  the correlates; brands with **<2,000 indexed pages mentioning them**
  were named just **3%** of the time.
- **Citations almost never point to your own site.** **99.99%** of
  category-research citations pointed to third-party domains; only **4 of
  150** brands earned a self-citation.
- **Brand naming rises sharply down-funnel.** On problem-awareness
  prompts brands were named in just **0.10%** of answers (their content
  cited but name omitted); on category-research prompts they were named
  **>12x** as often.
- **Off-site citation shape is vertical-specific.** Legal → a few
  prestige directories; SaaS → scattered across 10,000+ domains.

## Caveats

- Vendor/sponsored report (Victorious, on SEJ); methodology described in
  prose, no per-vertical raw tables or coefficient CIs published.
- "Recognition" (describe-the-brand probe) and "mention" (buying-stage
  answer) are the report's own constructs — the 96%/89% contrast depends
  on those definitions.
- Correlational, not causal; the correlations are brand-level.
