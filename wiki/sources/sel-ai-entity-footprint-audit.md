# How to Audit Your AI Entity Footprint

Rich Sanger, *Search Engine Land*, July 27, 2026.
<https://searchengineland.com/audit-ai-entity-footprint-483524>

Same author as [[richsanger-ai-overview-patent-insights]].

## What it is

A framework — the **"AI entity footprint audit"** — for evaluating how well AI
systems understand an organization from its collective public digital presence,
rather than auditing individual marketing assets one at a time. The core reframe:
stop asking "is this asset optimized?" and start asking "does everything
together help AI understand our identity, capabilities, and trustworthiness?"

## Key takeaways

- **Six audit dimensions**: Identity, Differentiation, Evidence, Consistency,
  Relationships, Specialization. (Full definitions in
  [[ai-entity-footprint-audit]].)
- **Four signal categories** feeding those dimensions: owned (site, schema,
  team profiles), customer (reviews, testimonials, case studies), third-party
  (press, awards, directories, certifications), and ecosystem (partnerships,
  associations, speaking, community).
- **0–5 scoring rubric** per dimension (0 = no evidence → 5 = exceptional
  understanding across independent sources), giving a repeatable, comparable
  score.
- **Concrete audit process**: start by literally asking an AI "tell me
  everything you know about [Business]" across multiple platforms, then
  cross-check website / Google Business Profile / reviews / third-party
  validation for consistency and gaps.
- **Industry variation** is in *evidence sources*, not the framework: local =
  GBP/reviews/citations; professional services = publications/speaking; SaaS =
  integrations/partnerships/ecosystem; ecommerce = product reviews/comparisons.
- Author ships an **"AI Entity Footprint Starter Audit" Custom GPT** for an
  automated first pass.

## Relationship to existing wiki claims

Corroborates and operationalizes, does not conflict:

- Extends [[brand-entity-seo-strategy]] — that playbook covers *building* brand
  entity signals (schema, `sameAs`, corroboration platforms); this adds an
  *audit/diagnostic* layer measuring whether those signals actually cohere into
  AI understanding.
- Complements [[ai-visibility-measurement-methodology]] — that measures whether
  a brand is *cited/gets traffic*; this measures whether a brand is *understood*
  (an upstream prerequisite). The "ask the AI to describe your business" probe
  is a qualitative-understanding check distinct from citation/impression counts.
- The **Consistency** dimension echoes the wiki's recurring off-site-consistency
  theme (cf. [[optimizing-for-the-agentic-web]], [[similarweb-how-to-win-the-race-for-gen-ai-search-2026]]).

  of signals AI uses to link and disambiguate the brand as an entity.
