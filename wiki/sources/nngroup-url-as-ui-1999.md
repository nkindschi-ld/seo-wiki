# URL as UI (Jakob Nielsen, NN/g)

**Citation:** Jakob Nielsen, "URL as UI," Nielsen Norman Group, 1999-03-20
(with 2005/2007 addenda). https://www.nngroup.com/articles/url-as-ui/

The **primary source** for the "Nielsen usability heuristics" already cited
secondhand (via [[visively-url-structure-fundamentals]]) in
[[url-structure-best-practices]]. A foundational, high-authority usability piece
framing the URL as part of the user interface — durable despite its 1999 date,
and the origin of the memorable/short/typeable/hackable/persistent framing.

## Key takeaways

- **Six requirements for usable URLs**: memorable+spellable domain, short,
  easy-to-type, structure-visualizing hierarchy, **hackable** (strip a segment
  to navigate up), and **persistent** (stable over time).
- **All-lowercase** — users can't reliably recall capitalization; mixed case
  invites failed recall/typos. (This actionable rule is *not* in the Dias
  playbook and is the main new tactic from this source.)
- **Persistence / no linkrot** — "never move pages"; use redirects only for
  genuinely temporary→permanent pointers. Matches Dias's design-for-persistence
  principle from the usability (not just link-equity) angle.
- **Short**: originally justified by an under-78-character email-line-wrap rule.
  The specific number is dated (email wrapping), but the "keep it short/typeable"
  principle holds.
- **Domain strategy**: `.com` for English-language worldwide sites; ccTLDs for
  local focus.
- **Credibility data point**: a 2007 Microsoft Research eye-tracking study found
  users spend **24% of gaze time on the URLs** in search results, especially
  when judging destination credibility — an early, human-behavior basis for
  clean/readable URLs that predates (and is consistent with) the AI-era
  clean-canonical-URL citation lift in [[otterly-url-ai-citations-study-2026]].

## Relationship to existing wiki claims

- **Primary-sources the Nielsen heuristics** in [[url-structure-best-practices]]
  (previously attributed via Dias). No conflict — same six heuristics.
- **Corroborates persistence** guidance in [[url-structure-best-practices]] and
  redirect discipline in [[content-pruning-playbook]] from a usability angle.
- **Adds the all-lowercase rule** as a new, concrete tactic.
- **Human-behavior antecedent** to the SERP/AI clean-URL findings in
  [[otterly-url-ai-citations-study-2026]] — readable URLs mattered for human
  credibility judgments (24% gaze time) long before they mattered for AI
  citations.

## Assessment

High authority (Nielsen/NN-g), foundational, and still broadly accurate. Only
the under-78-character email-wrap justification is genuinely dated; the
underlying principles (short, lowercase, hackable, persistent) remain standard.
No conflicts.
