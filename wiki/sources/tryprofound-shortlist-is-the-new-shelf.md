# The Shortlist is the New Shelf (Profound)

**Citation:** Indig, Kevin; Van Buskirk, Eric; Singh, Jasman. "The
Shortlist is the New Shelf." Profound Blog, 2026-07-14.
https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/the-shortlist-is-the-new-shelf (56
participants, 221 codable tasks, video/think-aloud session study +
6,882 ChatGPT citation analysis across 36 queries.)

## Key takeaways

- **Visibility-to-purchase correlation (new data type for this wiki)**:
  chosen brands had ~2x the share of voice of rejected brands in
  ChatGPT's answers (24% vs. 11%), correlation 0.57 — the first source
  in this wiki connecting AI *visibility* directly to an actual
  *purchase decision outcome*, one step downstream of
  [[ai-visibility-correlation-factors]]'s "what makes a brand appear at
  all" correlations.
- **Correlation strength is category-dependent, not universal**:
  ranges from 0.97 (grocery, near-deterministic) to -0.98 (coaching,
  reversed) — visibility optimization's payoff varies sharply by
  category, likely tracking commoditization vs. personalization/trust
  intensity.
- **Near-total no-click behavior at the purchase-decision stage**:
  92.8% of tasks ended with no web click; 48.9% accepted the
  recommendation outright; only 3.2% verified externally — sharper,
  task-level confirmation of the zero-click theme already in
  [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]] and [[aio-ctr-impact]].
- **High, unverified trust**: 81.9% reported increased decision
  confidence, 91.1% rated ChatGPT's information 4-5/5 reliable, despite
  almost never checking it externally.
- **Comparison grids are the dominant decision surface**: held
  attention in 35.9% of tasks (feature grids +15% more) — more than
  prose answers.
- **Four framing levers control outcomes independent of raw
  visibility**: appearing in the grid at all, a clear "best for X"
  label, accurate/current pricing, and plainly disclosed downsides.
- **Unknown brands can still win a shortlist slot** when presented with
  clear positioning — familiarity isn't a prerequisite for
  consideration at this stage.
- **Caveats (stated by authors)**: session-level analysis (not
  longitudinal); think-aloud doesn't perfectly mirror real browsing;
  thin categories (<5 brands) are directional only; sample skews toward
  experienced daily AI users (66%), which may not generalize to
  infrequent users.

## Relationship to existing wiki claims

- **Extends** [[ai-visibility-correlation-factors]] with a genuinely
  new outcome variable (purchase/selection, not just mention
  visibility) — no conflict, since that page's Spearman correlations
  measure appearance, and this source measures the next stage
  (appearance → selection).
- **Sharpens** [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]]'s and [[aio-ctr-impact]]'s
  zero-click theme with task-level purchase-decision data (92.8%
  no-click) rather than aggregate impression/click ratios.
- **Distinct from, complements** [[listicles-in-ai-search]]: that page
  covers third-party listicle content getting *cited*; this source
  covers ChatGPT's own live-generated comparison grid as the decision
  surface, a related but separate mechanism.
- New concept page [[ai-shortlist-effect]] created to house this
  source's findings, given the distinct methodology (session/video
  study of live purchase decisions) and new correlation-to-outcome
  data type not covered by any existing page.
