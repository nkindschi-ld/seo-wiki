John Mueller (Google Search Relations), "Top ways to ensure your
content performs well in Google's AI experiences on Search" (Google
Search Central Blog).

The **earliest official Google guidance in this wiki on AI-search
optimization** — predates and is superseded-in-detail-but-consistent-
with [[google-ai-features-appearance-guide]] (2025-12) and
[[google-ai-optimization-guide]] (2026-06), Google's later, more
detailed official docs. Confirms this wiki's existing claims (helpful/
people-first content, technical crawlability requirements, the
nosnippet/data-nosnippet/max-snippet/noindex preview controls, and
"AI Overview clicks are higher quality/more engaged") were consistent
with Google's position as early as May 2025, not a later reversal or
addition. No conflicts.

## Key takeaways

- **Historical/precursor value**: establishes that Google's core
  "helpful, people-first content + technical crawlability" framing for
  AI Overviews/AI Mode dates to at least May 2025 — useful for dating
  how long-standing this guidance is, corroborating rather than
  extending [[e-e-a-t-and-page-quality]] and [[how-google-search-works]].
- **Preview controls** (`nosnippet`, `data-nosnippet`, `max-snippet`,
  `noindex`) — identical to what's already documented in
  [[controlling-ai-feature-inclusion]]; this is the earlier source for
  the same guidance.
- **"Higher quality/more engaged" AI Overview clicks** — the earlier,
  less-detailed version of the claim already in
  [[generative-engine-optimization]] (attributed there to
  [[google-ai-features-appearance-guide]]).
- **New, minor addition**: "make sure structured data matches the
  visible content" — an explicit Google tip not previously stated
  verbatim in this wiki's technical/structured-data guidance. Added to
  [[technical-seo-audit-checklist]].
- **New, minor addition**: "provide a great page experience" for AI
  traffic specifically — cluttered navigation, cross-device display,
  latency, and distinguishing main content from other page content.
  Framed as applying to AI-search visitors specifically, not just
  classic Search. Added to [[technical-seo-audit-checklist]].
- Multimodal search (images/video + Merchant Center/Business Profile
  freshness) already covered by [[google-ai-optimization-guide]]; no
  change needed.
