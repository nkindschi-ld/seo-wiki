Rachel Handley (Semrush), "What Is ChatGPT Search & How Does It Work?"
(contributors: Christine Skopec, Simon Fogg, Selina Scheumann).

A Semrush explainer on ChatGPT Search mechanics and optimization.
Higher rigor than the recent marketing-agency guides ingested
(SingleGrain, First Page Sage) — a research-team byline with named
contributors, though still largely explanatory rather than a primary
data study. Most of its optimization tactics (question-focused content,
NLP-friendly writing, schema markup, entity mentions, reputation
building) restate existing [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]]
guidance. Filed for its mechanics detail and a few genuinely new data
points/dates.

## Key takeaways

- **ChatGPT Search's three retrieval sources**: OpenAI's own crawled
  index (`OAI-SearchBot`), **Bing's search index via a Microsoft
  partnership**, and direct data partnerships with news/data providers.
  The Bing-dependency detail is new — extends
  [[generative-engine-optimization]]'s existing Copilot-follows-Bing
  observation to ChatGPT Search specifically.
- **Launch history**: SearchGPT prototype introduced 2024-07-25;
  integrated into ChatGPT as ChatGPT Search on 2024-10-31. Not
  previously dated in this wiki — added to `wiki/timeline.md`.
- **Earlier traffic-ratio baseline**: Google ~28x more traffic than
  ChatGPT alone, as of November 2024 — an earlier snapshot and
  narrower comparison than this wiki's existing 345x
  ([[ahrefs-b2b-seo-statistics-2025]]) and "1/1,000th"
  ([[sparktoro-influence-happens-everywhere-2026]]) figures. Not a
  contradiction (different scope/time), but a useful earlier data
  point for tracking the gap's trajectory. Added to
  [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]].
- **Gartner forecast**: brands' organic search traffic predicted to
  halve by 2028 as generative AI search adoption grows. A forward-
  looking prediction, not a measurement — flagged as such. Added to
  [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]].
- **Practitioner anecdote**: a consultant observed ChatGPT Search "may
  rank smaller websites more fairly than Google" — directionally
  consistent with (not independent confirmation of)
  [[generative-engine-optimization]]'s measured "+115% for a rank-5
  site, -30% for the rank-1 site" GEO-equity finding. Added as
  corroborating anecdote there.
- Optimization tactics (question-focused content, schema markup, NLP-
  friendly writing, entity mentions, digital PR/reviews/directories for
  reputation) restate existing, more detailed wiki guidance — no
  changes needed for those sections.
