# How to Optimize Content for AI Search: The Complete Guide (Otterly.ai, 2026)

**Citation:** Thomas Peham. "How to Optimize Content for AI Search: The
Complete Guide (2026)." Otterly.ai Blog, 2026-02-16.
https://otterly.ai/blog/how-to-optimize-content-for-ai-search/

## What this source is

A tactics/recommendations guide, not a standalone data study. Its seven
core tactics (answer-first structure, factual density, AI-crawler
robots.txt allowlist, schema markup, citation-hook content patterns,
platform-specific optimization, freshness cadence, third-party
authority) **restate tactics already well-covered in this wiki**
([[geo-content-optimization-tactics]], [[robots-txt-strategy]],
[[e-e-a-t-and-page-quality]]) — not individually re-filed here. The
guide mixes primary sources (the GEO founding paper, OtterlyAI's own
experiments) with **secondhand citations of other outlets (BrightEdge,
McKinsey, Previsible, Monash University) with no links or disclosed
methodology given for any of them** — treat those specific numbers with
the same caution already applied to other undisclosed-methodology
sources in this wiki.

## What's new or notable (the rest restates existing tactics)

- **A contradicting counter-claim on organic/AI-citation overlap**:
  this source states (via BrightEdge, no link given) that the overlap
  between AI Overview citations and top-10 organic rankings **grew**
  from 32.3% to 54.5%. This lands in the *opposite direction* from
  [[rankability-where-seo-is-going-2026]]'s claim (already flagged as
  unverified in [[ai-citation-landscape]]) that this same overlap
  **collapsed** from ~75% to 17-38% over a similar window. Neither
  source discloses the methodology behind its specific figure, so this
  doesn't resolve anything — it's a second unverified voice, pointing
  the opposite way from the first. The 54.5% figure here does land
  closer to the existing, better-sourced 76%-top-10 figure from
  [[ahrefs-b2b-seo-statistics-2025]] than Rankability's 17-38% range
  does, for what that's worth given none of these three are directly
  comparable studies.
- **FAQ schema citation-lift experiment (OtterlyAI's own, primary)**:
  implementing FAQ schema showed a 350% increase in AI citations in
  one of their own experiments (2,379 vs. 529 citations). A concrete,
  first-party number not previously in this wiki's schema-markup
  guidance.
- **Freshness — another voice on an existing unresolved conflict**:
  "sources cited in AI responses are 26% fresher than traditional
  search results" (Monash University, no link given) — adds to the
  freshness-matters side of [[ai-citation-landscape]]'s existing
  Conflicting Evidence on optimal content age, without resolving it.
- **Daily query volume by engine (January 2026, no source disclosed
  for this specific table)**: Google AI Overviews/AI Mode ~15B/day,
  ChatGPT ~2.5B/day, Gemini ~46M/day, Copilot ~33M/day, DeepSeek
  ~29M/day, Perplexity ~26M/day — a new absolute-scale data point for
  [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]], though undisclosed sourcing means it
  should sit alongside that page's other absolute-user-count figures
  as directional rather than authoritative.
