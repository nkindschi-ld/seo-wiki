# AI Platform Citation Patterns (Profound, Nick Lafferty, 2025-06-05, updated Aug 2025)

**Citation:** Lafferty, Nick (Profound). "AI Platform Citation
Patterns: How ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity Source
Information." 2025-06-05, updated August 2025. Data period Aug 2024–Jun
2025, 680M citations.
https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns

## Key takeaways

- **ChatGPT**: Wikipedia-led (7.8% of total citations, 47.9% of its
  top 10) — an authoritative-knowledge-base/established-media lean.
  Reddit a minor secondary source (1.8%).
- **Perplexity**: Reddit-led (6.6% of total, 46.7% of top 10) —
  "community-driven information" framing.
- **Google AI Overviews**: Reddit (2.2%) and YouTube (1.9%) lead, with
  Quora (1.5%) and LinkedIn (1.3%) close behind — a "balanced
  social-professional mix," more evenly spread than the other two
  platforms' top-10 lists.
- **Domain TLD mix** (aggregate, not per-platform): .com 80.41%, .org
  11.29%, ccTLDs ~3.5% combined, .io/.ai described as growing.
- Explicit strategic conclusion: platform-specific sourcing preferences
  mean "a one-size-fits-all approach to AI visibility cannot succeed."

## What this updates in the wiki

Mostly agrees with and adds a new data point to existing
[[ai-citation-landscape]] findings — but surfaces two **magnitude
tensions** with prior sources that are flagged, not resolved:

1. Perplexity's Reddit-heavy (6.6%/46.7% of top 10) "community-driven"
   profile here reads in tension with
   [[brightedge-ai-search-same-brands-different-sources]]'s
   "research librarian," low-UGC (1.5%) characterization of Perplexity.
2. Google AI Overviews' YouTube share here (1.9%) is an order of
   magnitude below brightedge's "single video platform = 10.6% of AI
   Overviews citations" figure.

Both are plausibly explained by different sampling windows/citation
definitions rather than a real behavioral shift, but neither source
resolves the other, so both are logged as open discrepancies rather
than a corrected claim.
