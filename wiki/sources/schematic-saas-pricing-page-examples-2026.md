# 6 Effective SaaS Pricing Page Examples and Why They Work (Schematic, 2026-10-15)

**Citation:** Echternacht, Ryan. "6 Effective SaaS Pricing Page Examples
and Why They Work." Schematic blog, 2026-10-15.
https://schematichq.com/blog/saas-pricing-page-examples

**Rigor: low to moderate, and tangential.** Vendor content (Schematic
sells a monetization, billing and entitlements platform; the last
section is a product pitch). About conversion and page design, not SEO
or AEO. No data beyond a Statista mobile-traffic figure (~62%). The
examples are descriptive teardowns, not tested results. Filed because
it's the conversion-side baseline for the pricing-page playbook.

## Key takeaways

- **Core elements:** a value-driven headline (not "Pricing Plans") whose
  sub-headline hints at the pricing model (free plan, per-seat,
  usage-based); 3–4 plans side by side; a monthly/annual toggle showing
  savings; a highlighted recommended plan; a full feature comparison
  table *below* the plan cards; trust signals; a FAQ near the bottom;
  optional competitor comparison.
- **FAQ topics to cover:** what happens after the trial ends,
  cancel/downgrade, hidden fees, tier usage limits, monthly vs. yearly
  billing, whether support is included. These match the
  "natural-language pricing questions" in [[ai-pricing-visibility-audit]].
- **Six examples:**
  - **Vercel:** simple three tiers up top, deep detail lower down,
    long transparent FAQ.
  - **PostHog:** e-commerce-checkout style, free plan + pay-as-you-go,
    per-product cost calculator.
  - **Supabase:** tiers plus usage add-ons; many features **link to the
    docs**; predictable instance-based compute pricing.
  - **Mintlify:** three plans, toggle on the middle tier, grouped
    feature sections.
  - **Otter.ai:** four tiers with limits (minutes, meeting length) shown
    in the cards.
  - **Linear:** four tiers, no FAQ because pricing is simple.
- **Mistakes:** too many plans, feature overload, hidden pricing
  ("avoid forcing users to contact sales teams just to see pricing"),
  poor mobile layout, no social proof, never revisiting pricing.

## Relevance to SEO/AEO

Indirect only. Supabase's pricing-to-docs links are the
"pricing page + billing docs that link to each other" pattern from
[[ai-pricing-visibility-audit]]. "Don't hide pricing" agrees with the AI
visibility evidence there (no public price → AI invents a range from
Reddit). Nothing here on schema, crawlability or search queries.
