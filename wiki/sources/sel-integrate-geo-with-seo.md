# How To Integrate GEO With SEO (Search Engine Land, Christina Adame)

**Citation:** Christina Adame, "How To Integrate GEO With SEO," Search
Engine Land, 2025-03-19. https://searchengineland.com/integrate-geo-seo-453351

No disclosed methodology or study — a practitioner synthesis/framework
piece (contributor byline, no named research team), quoting Crystal
Carter (Wix). Treat framework naming and directional guidance as useful,
but individual claims as unverified unless independently corroborated
elsewhere in this wiki.

## Key takeaways

- **"Retrievability" as a named fourth pillar** beyond classic
  crawlability/indexability/rankability — attributed to Crystal Carter
  (Wix): "how effectively AI can access, interpret, and prioritize
  information about your brand when forming responses." Net-new named
  framing for a concept this wiki already covers piecemeal (retrieval
  rank as gatekeeper, technical crawlability checklist) but hadn't
  labeled as a fourth SEO-pipeline stage.
- **A "Presence / Recognition / Accessibility" three-pillar
  retrievability framework** — presence (consistent brand mentions in
  AI training/retrieval sources), recognition (credibility via
  consistent contextual mentions and trusted-entity associations),
  accessibility (info structured for AI retrieval). **Naming collision,
  not a factual conflict**: this wiki already uses "Presence" in
  [[sej-the-consensus-gap]]'s Presence/Portability/Concentration
  framework, with a different meaning (% of prompts where a brand
  appears in *any* engine, there) than this source's "consistent
  mentions shaping training data" sense — flagged so the two aren't
  conflated when both are referenced.
- **Authority-signal shift claim**: backlinks (classic SEO authority
  signal) give way to contextual relevance, entity associations, and
  brand-mention consistency for AI. Directionally consistent with
  existing wiki findings (AirOps' near-null backlink correlation,
  Ahrefs' weak backlink/DR correlation) but stated here as an
  unqualified claim rather than a measured finding.
- **On-page/off-page/technical GEO-SEO integration checklists** —
  substantially restate tactics already in [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]],
  [[entity-based-seo-implementation]], and [[brand-entity-seo-strategy]]
  (topical authority/entity clusters, internal linking for entity
  reinforcement, Wikipedia/Wikidata/Knowledge Graph presence, structured
  data, AI crawler allowlisting). No new tactics in these sections beyond
  what's already documented.
- **Concrete measurement additions**: a specific GA4 regex filter
  pattern for capturing AI-referral traffic (ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity/
  Copilot/etc. domains), and a Google-Sheets-plus-ChatGPT-API pattern for
  automating AI-citation/mention monitoring — operational detail beyond
  this wiki's existing measurement guidance.
- **44%+ branded-search-terms stat** (cited to Rand Fishkin) —
  corroborates the wiki's existing 44.19% branded-search figure
  (SparkToro, via [[iloveseo-brand-seo-and-ai]]); same underlying
  claim, not independent confirmation.
- **ChatGPT reached 400M weekly active users by February 2025**,
  described as doubling in the prior six months — an earlier data point
  than this wiki's existing 800M+ weekly-user figure
  ([[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]]), useful as a dated timeline entry
  rather than a competing current figure.
