# Claude AI Citations Study: How to Get Cited in 2026 (Otterly.ai)

**Citation:** Rick Tousseyn. "Claude AI Citations Study: How to Get Cited in
2026." Otterly.ai Blog, 2026-06-30.
https://otterly.ai/blog/claude-ai-citation-study/

## What this source is

A June 2026 citation-domain analysis of Claude's citing behavior: **379,321
Claude citations** across **16,406 unique domains** in **11 source
categories**, focused on SaaS and technology queries. Same author (Rick
Tousseyn) and methodology family as earlier Otterly studies, but
narrowed to a single engine (Claude only) and single query vertical
(SaaS/tech). Tracks which domains Claude cites and their source
classification, not metadata correlations or engagement signals like the
earlier studies.

## Key findings

- **Brand/first-party content dominates Claude's citations far more than
  any other engine**: 64.0% of all citations come from brand/company-owned
  websites (10,842 unique domains). Compare to the ChatGPT/Perplexity/
  Gemini citation mix documented elsewhere in this wiki, where no single
  source type approaches 64% — Claude is orders of magnitude more
  brand-centric.
- **Top-10 domains account for only 9.5% of all citations**: Claude's
  citation distribution is exceptionally dispersed. 32.9% of cited
  domains appear only once in the entire dataset. This is the opposite
  of the "citation power law" pattern found on other surfaces
  ([[otterly-url-ai-citations-study-2026]]'s 15.8% of URLs → 50% of
  citations) — Claude distributes citations more evenly across a long
  tail of sources.
- **Social media is nearly negligible for Claude**: only 0.9% of all
  citations come from any social platform combined (37 domains total).
  Within that 0.9%, LinkedIn accounts for 43.1%; Reddit was cited **0
  times**; Twitter/other platforms are effectively absent. This is a
  stark contrast to earlier findings that Claude's broader citation
  pattern is relatively balanced across source types — at the platform
  level, Claude is extremely selective.
- **News/Media is the second-largest source category**: 14.9% of
  citations (1,688 domains) — significant but well below brand. Blogs/
  personal, education, and other categories are single digits.
- **Citation patterns remained stable throughout June 2026**: no
  significant drift over the month, enabling reliable planning/forecasting
  based on these distributions.

## Relationship to existing wiki claims

- **Dramatically extends the Claude UGC-aversion finding**: per
  [[ai-citation-landscape]]'s section "Wikipedia, Reddit, and YouTube
  diverge sharply by provider," Claude never cites YouTube (0%) and cites
  Reddit 0% — the wiki previously described this as a data point but now
  this source quantifies that social media *as a category* is 0.9% of
  Claude's citations, making it immaterial to Claude strategy. Earlier
  findings described Claude as an outlier among the five engines; this
  source shows just how extreme that outlier is.
- **Consistent with Claude's "cites far less often but much more deeply"
  profile**: per [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]], Claude cites in
  only 55% of responses (vs. 96% for ChatGPT) but averages **13 citations
  when it does**, more than twice ChatGPT's depth. The brand-heavy mix
  now makes sense: deep, reference-grade sources (brand/official docs,
  academic/institutional content) rather than broad engagement-driven
  platforms.
- **Largest-scale Claude citation study to date**: earlier findings about
  Claude's sourcing "personality" ([[brightedge-ai-search-same-brands-different-sources]],
  [[ai-citation-landscape]]) were derived from cross-engine studies that
  included Claude as one of 3-5 engines. This is the first single-engine,
  Claude-focused dataset at scale (379K citations).
- **New to this wiki**: the precise, quantified breakdown of Claude's
  source-type mix and the finding that social media is <1% of citations.
