Eric Siu, "Google AI Overviews: The Ultimate Guide to Ranking in 2025"
(Single Grain).

A marketing-agency AIO-optimization round-up. No disclosed methodology,
sample size, or measurement window for any of its statistics — lower
evidentiary weight than this wiki's primary studies (Seer Interactive,
Semrush/Datos, AirOps). Its 15 tactics mostly restate existing wiki
guidance (schema markup, E-E-A-T, content clusters, PAA-based headers,
long-tail targeting — see [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]]). Filed
primarily to record two genuine conflicts with higher-rigor sources
already in the wiki, plus two lower-confidence additions.

## Key takeaways

- **Conflict — AIO prevalence**: claims 50%+ of all search results now
  show an AI Overview. Contradicts [[semrush-ai-overviews-study-2025]]
  (10M+ keywords), which shows prevalence *settling back down* to
  15.69% by November 2025 after a July 2025 peak, and
  [[sel-what-is-generative-engine-optimization-geo-2026]]'s independent
  "at least 16%" figure from the same rough window. Logged as unresolved
  Conflicting Evidence on [[aio-ctr-impact]] — current best guess favors
  the ~15-16% figures given disclosed methodology and sample size on
  both.
- **Conflict — local-query AIO presence**: claims only ~7% of local
  queries trigger an AI Overview. Directly conflicts with this wiki's
  existing Seer-sourced figure that "near me" queries trigger AIOs
  76.9% of the time (25M impressions). Logged as unresolved Conflicting
  Evidence on [[aio-ctr-impact]] — likely a definitional mismatch
  (local-vertical categories vs. "near me" query format) rather than a
  clean contradiction, but neither source clarifies which.
- **Unverified addition — position-based CTR decline**: position #1
  organic CTR 28%→19%, position #2 20.83%→12.60%, average -17.92%
  across positions 1-5. A different metric than this wiki's existing
  citation-status CTR data (cited vs. not-cited, not pre/post-AIO by
  SERP position), so not directly contradicted — added to
  [[aio-ctr-impact]] flagged as unverified, no disclosed methodology.
- **Unverified addition — query-length trigger correlation**: queries
  of 8+ words claimed 7x more likely to trigger an AIO. Directionally
  consistent with (but a different metric than) the existing long-tail
  skew finding in [[semrush-ai-overviews-study-2025]]. Added to
  [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] flagged as unverified.
- **Unverified addition — direct-answer word count**: recommends a
  50-70 word self-contained direct answer immediately after the H1.
  No other source in this wiki specifies a word-count range for this
  tactic. Added to [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] as a reasonable
  starting heuristic, not a measured optimum.
- Everything else (schema types, content clusters, E-E-A-T, long-tail
  targeting, internal linking, freshness, local optimization, common
  mistakes, staged 30/90/6-month action plan) restates existing wiki
  guidance without adding new information — no changes needed for
  those sections.
