Leigh McKenzie, "Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): How to Win AI
Mentions" (Search Engine Land).

A general GEO explainer/primer. Most of its content — the SEO-vs-GEO
framing, entity-clarity, extractable-content, and multi-platform
principles — restates ground this wiki already covers in more depth
(see [[generative-engine-optimization]],
[[geo-content-optimization-tactics]], [[wix-generative-engine-optimization]]).
Filed primarily for its few new data points, not as a foundational
source.

## Key takeaways

- **Citation-source volatility: 40-60% of cited sources change
  month-to-month.** A new, specific churn figure not previously in this
  wiki — extends [[ai-citation-landscape]]'s citation-composition data
  with a stability/volatility dimension (that page documents *what*
  gets cited and by which engines, but not *how often the same sources
  keep getting cited over time*).
- **Absolute AI-tool user-base scale**: ChatGPT >800M weekly users;
  Google's Gemini app >750M monthly users. This is a different metric
  (raw active users) than [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]]'s clickstream
  visit-share framing ("1/1,000th the size of the press coverage") —
  not a contradiction, since a platform can have a huge absolute user
  base while still being tiny relative to Google's overall search
  volume. Both are worth keeping: one measures absolute reach, the
  other measures relative share of attention.
- **Tally case study**: the form-builder tool Tally names ChatGPT as
  its "#1 referral source." A concrete anecdote that AI referral can
  dominate for an individual brand/niche even while AI tools remain
  small in aggregate clickstream share — a useful individual-brand
  counterpoint to [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]]'s aggregate framing,
  not a rebuttal of it.
- **~16% of all searches show an AI Overview**, higher for
  comparison/high-intent queries — broadly corroborates (doesn't
  extend) [[aio-ctr-impact]]'s existing Semrush-sourced prevalence data
  (6.49% → peaked 24.61% → settled 15.69% through 2025); no new
  information here, just an independent rough match on the settled
  figure.
- Everything else (SEO-vs-GEO comparison table, five core principles,
  extractable-content checklist, entity-clarity-across-schema/LinkedIn/
  directories, owned-vs-earned presence, two-dashboard measurement
  framing) restates existing wiki claims — filed as a supporting
  citation, no updates needed to those sections.
