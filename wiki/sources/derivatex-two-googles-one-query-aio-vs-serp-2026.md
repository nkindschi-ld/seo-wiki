# DerivateX — "Two Googles, One Query": AIO vs. SERP Source Overlap (Sharma, 2026)

**Citation:** Apoorv Sharma, "Two Googles, One Query: Google AI Overviews share only
35% of their sources with the search results below them," DerivateX Research, July 6,
2026. https://derivatex.agency/report/google-aio-vs-serp-benchmark/

A benchmark comparing Google's **AI Overview (AIO) citations** against the **top-10
organic SERP** captured for the *same query in the same session* — i.e. how much
Google's two on-screen discovery layers actually agree. 5th in DerivateX's AI-citation
research series.

## Methodology (fully disclosed — weight as directional)

100 buyer-intent queries across 20 B2B software categories (5/category); 1,259 AIO
citations vs. 1,000 top-10 SERP results; 445 AIO-recommended products matched to SERP.
Single-session captures, June–July 2026. **Limitations acknowledged:** B2B-software
vertical only (absolute percentages won't generalize), AIO non-deterministic
(geo/personalization/time), single-session (no run-to-run variance), product overlap
measured conservatively, category averages from just 5 queries each. Treat as
directional, not definitive.

## Key takeaways

- **AIO↔SERP source overlap = 35%** (65% of AIO citations don't rank in Google's own
  top-10). Asymmetric: only 42% of top-10 domains get cited in the AIO above them.
  Two "separate discovery layers with separate content preferences."
- **Product-recommendation overlap = 28%** (72% AIO-exclusive); range 0% (32/100
  queries) to 100% (5/100).
- **When shared, quality is high: 72% of overlapping sources rank top-5, median #4;**
  only 2 shared instances from #9–10. Ranking is **necessary-but-not-sufficient**, and
  specifically **top-5** — page-2 almost never crosses over.
- **YouTube–Reddit inversion (largest gap):** AIO cites YouTube 7.3× more than SERP
  ranks it (51 vs. 7 of 100 queries); SERP ranks Reddit 1.9× more than AIO cites it.
  Source-mix: AIO heavier on listicles (63.4% vs. 55.4%) and video (9.0% vs. 2.3%),
  lighter on Reddit (4.7% vs. 11.0%) and review sites (5.0% vs. 9.9%).
- **Category divergence tracks listicle-ecosystem maturity:** ~20% overlap (help desk,
  project management) up to 62% (QuickBooks hosting, the lone category tracking
  Google). Mature "best-of" listicle categories diverge most.
- **No direct CTR/traffic data** — claims are inferential. Recommends tracking AIO
  citations and Google rankings as **distinct KPIs**.
