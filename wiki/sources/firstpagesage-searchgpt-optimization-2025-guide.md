Evan Bailyn, "SearchGPT Optimization: 2026 Guide" (First Page Sage).

A marketing-agency guide presenting the author's own model of
"SearchGPT's recommendation algorithm" — no disclosed methodology,
sample size, or underlying study for any of its five ranking factors
or six tactics (similar evidentiary tier to
[[singlegrain-google-ai-overviews-ultimate-guide-2025]]). Most of its
content restates existing wiki guidance (listicle placement, review
platforms, social sentiment monitoring, Wikipedia presence — see
[[geo-content-optimization-tactics]]); filed for a few genuinely new
tactic names and as another data point in an existing unresolved
conflict, not as a rigorous new study.

## Key takeaways

- **Awards/credentials/accreditations as an authority signal** — not
  previously in this wiki. Added as a new, explicitly lower-confidence
  section in [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] (no disclosed
  methodology).
- **Expanded review-platform list**: Clutch, CNET, TrustPilot, Better
  Business Bureau — added alongside the existing G2/Glassdoor/
  Sitejabber list in [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]].
- **Directory placements beyond Wikipedia**: Hoovers, Bloomberg — minor
  addition to the existing Wikipedia-presence tactic.
- **"Increase Google Domain Authority" claim** (SearchGPT/ChatGPT
  references top Google results, so classic ranking feeds AI
  visibility) — adds a data point to the "supported by" side of
  [[ai-visibility-correlation-factors]]'s existing unresolved
  Conflicting Evidence on domain/brand authority vs. AI citation. Does
  not resolve that conflict (AirOps' page-level null/inverse finding
  still stands on the other side); just one more voice on the
  authority-matters side.
- **Customer/usage-metrics as a substitute signal for newer brands**
  lacking list placements — a minor framing addition, not independently
  verified.
- Everything else (authoritative list mentions, social sentiment as a
  lower-weighted-but-difference-making factor, review solicitation)
  restates existing, more rigorously-sourced wiki guidance — no changes
  needed for those sections.
