# ChatGPT Fan-Out Queries: Key Findings and Analysis

Author: Lily Ray
Published: 2026-08-17
Source: https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/what-we-can-learn-from-evolving-chatgpt

## Executive Summary

OpenAI is increasingly using the `site:` search operator in ChatGPT's fan-out queries to retrieve information from trusted, high-authority domains. This strategy appears designed to reduce spammy content in AI-generated responses while improving answer quality.

## Major Data Points

**Model Changes:**
- Share of single fan-out queries dropped from 94.0% to 43.5% with ChatGPT 5.6 default
- Average retrieved sources roughly doubled from ~12 to ~24 pages
- Site: operator usage increased from 0.3% to 23% of fan-outs (David Konitzny/Peec AI)
- Chris Long/Nectiv found average fan-out queries per prompt jumped from 2.17 to 7.61

**Content Preferences:**
- Product pages now comprise 16.39% of retrieved pages
- Listicles, how-to guides, and comparison pages losing retrieval share
- High-authority domains dominating citations: Reddit, Wikipedia, Forbes, Consumer Reports

**Citation vs. Retrieval Divergence:**
- Unique domains cited per response dropped from 19 to 15 despite higher retrieval counts
- Suganthan Mohanadasan found: brands named in initial queries cited 68.9% of the time, versus 2.1% for pages only fetched

## Search Behavior Patterns

ChatGPT demonstrates query-specific domain preferences:

- **Opinions/Reviews:** Reddit and specific subreddits
- **Legal/Health (YMYL):** .gov domains exclusively in some tests
- **Product specs/pricing:** Brand official domains (site:sephora.com, site:costco.com)

The model "decides before it searches"—initial queries contained brand names users never mentioned in 21 of 27 product category tests.

## Risks Identified

**Domain Confusion:** Malte Landwehr discovered instances where ChatGPT constructed `site:` queries against incorrect domains—searching site:census.com for the startup Census instead of getcensus.com.

**Security Concern:** Netcraft found roughly one-third of brand login links generated by LLMs pointed to domains brands didn't own; approximately 29% targeted unregistered or parked domains.

## Word Frequency in Queries

Top unigrams in recent fan-outs: "site:", "official," "gov" (Chris Long/Conductor data)

## SEO Recommendations

1. Build brand recognition so ChatGPT naturally considers your company first
2. Ensure key facts (pricing, specs) appear in crawlable HTML, not JavaScript or images
3. Clarify official domain status through strategic title tag/meta description language
4. Avoid targeting individual fan-out queries to prevent spam penalties
5. Monitor site: search impressions in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
6. Recognize Reddit as category-shaping despite low citation rates (Dan Petrovic: ChatGPT discards Reddit retrievals ~99% of the time)

## Methodology Notes

Data compiled from multiple independent researchers using different tools:
- Peec AI and Profound (tracked prompts)
- Resoneo Chrome plugin and FanoutFox (visible query tracking)
- OpenAI API extraction (Chris Long)
- Network traffic analysis (Suganthan Mohanadasan)

Sample sizes and collection methods vary, explaining some discrepancies between studies.
