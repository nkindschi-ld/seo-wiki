## Definition

MOFU (middle-of-funnel) keywords = search terms used in the **evaluation stage** —
actively comparing options, problem understood, brand not yet chosen. Sits between
TOFU (awareness) and BOFU (decision). MOFU terms typically have 3+ words with
"consideration modifiers."

| Stage | B2C (coffee) | B2B (inventory software) |
|---|---|---|
| TOFU | "morning routine ideas" | "coffee inventory tracking" |
| MOFU | "coffee subscription pros and cons" | "inventory management for coffee business" |
| BOFU | "[Brand] reviews" | "[Software] pricing" |

## Why MOFU matters (5 benefits)

Higher-quality leads than TOFU (qualified intent); capture prospects earlier than
BOFU-only competitors; traffic/conversion balance (more volume than BOFU, better
conversion than TOFU); lower PPC/CPC than BOFU; builds topical authority.

## Five MOFU keyword types (the useful taxonomy)

1. **Comparison** — "X vs Y" ("HubSpot vs. Salesforce"). Explain who each serves,
   balanced pros/cons, use-case recommendations.
2. **Best-of ranked lists** — "best/top/ultimate" ("best email marketing tools for
   nonprofits"). Pricing, pros/cons, use cases, visuals; update annually.
3. **Feature-driven** — specific functionality ("CRM with WhatsApp integration").
   Explain feature value and which use cases benefit.
4. **Industry-specific solutions** — problem + solution + industry ("accounting
   software for freelancers"). Especially effective B2B; lower competition, smaller
   volume.
5. **Pain-solution** — problem-focused ("how to reduce churn in SaaS"). Offer multiple
   solution tiers (DIY → professional) to integrate offerings naturally.

## Content best practices

Match intent to stage (not TOFU education, not BOFU hard-sell); honest balanced
comparisons with pricing; structured/scannable formats (comparison tables, bullet
pros/cons, feature grids, FAQ); E-E-A-T trust signals (case studies, testing
methodology, author credentials, updated dates, authoritative citations); soft CTAs
("14-day free trial," "download guide," "book consultation"), not hard sells.
"The art of MOFU content is being helpful first, promotional second."

## Funnel-stage internal linking

- **BOFU links** → product pages, pricing, reviews.
- **TOFU links** → educational/problem content.
- **Lateral links** → related MOFU content.

## Measurement

- **GA4 Path Exploration** to find MOFU **assisted** conversions: Explore → Path
  explorations → enter MOFU page titles in Step +1 → see downstream events / conversion
  patterns 2–3 pages after the MOFU touchpoint.
- **Custom events** (e.g. thank-you page view) marked as Key Events.
- **Semrush Position Tracking** (visibility score, SERP features, competitors).
- **Engagement benchmarks:** MOFU should show higher time-on-page, lower bounce, more
  pages/session, greater scroll depth than TOFU.
- **B2C metrics** (AOV, decision time, cart abandonment) vs. **B2B metrics** (sales
  cycle length, deal size, SQL rate, demo attendance).
- **Multi-touch attribution**: time-decay, position-based, data-driven — because MOFU
  is a "long-term investment" (days to months to convert).

## Data points

Coffee-subscription Semrush examples ("morning routine ideas" 1,300 vol / KD 32);
"best coffee subscription" CPC $4.84 vs. ~$2.05 food-industry average.

## Tools

Google Keyword Planner, Semrush (featured throughout), Ahrefs. Evaluate volume,
keyword difficulty, and CPC (CPC gap helps identify BOFU vs. MOFU).
