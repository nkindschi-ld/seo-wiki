# SEO Case Study: How We Influenced the ChatGPT Search Results

Source: https://gofishdigital.com/blog/seo-case-study-how-we-influenced-the-chatgpt-search-results/
Author: Chris Long, VP of Marketing, Go Fish Digital
Publish Date: July 25, 2025

---

## Premise

ChatGPT Search (launched October 2024) pulls from Bing search results
rather than purely internal training data, meaning it can be influenced
through strategic content optimization the same way classic SEO
influences Bing/Google rankings.

## Case study

**Initial observation**: Go Fish Digital monitored how ChatGPT Search
displayed the company for the query "digital PR agency" — a historically
strong-performing term for them. Competitors' ChatGPT Search listings
included a "Notable Clients" section; Go Fish Digital's own listing did
not.

**Methodology**: identified an existing listicle article that ChatGPT
Search frequently cited for this query. Modified that content by adding
a "Notable Clients" section, structured as a bullet list organized as
"key value pairs" — a format chosen specifically to appeal to how AI
indexing/extraction systems parse content.

**Result**: after approximately one week, ChatGPT Search began
displaying notable clients in Go Fish Digital's search result listing
for the target query.

## Key claims and recommendations

- "When ChatGPT uses the Search function, right now you CAN have the
  ability to influence the output."
- Two requirements for this kind of influence: (1) possess content/
  articles that already feed into ChatGPT's citations for the target
  query, (2) adjust that content strategically to control how the brand
  is presented within it.
- Future testing area noted by the team: whether ChatGPT can be
  influenced to display awards and "years active" information directly
  in its outputs, using the same approach.

## Industry commentary themes

Content structuring for AI-citation preference; GEO ("GenAI Optimizer")
as an evolution beyond traditional SEO; AI-driven search as the next
visibility frontier.
