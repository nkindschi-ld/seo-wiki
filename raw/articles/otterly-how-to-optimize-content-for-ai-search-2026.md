## Core definition

AI search optimization (GEO) = structuring/enhancing content so AI-powered search engines cite it as a source in generated responses.

## Seven primary tactics (restate existing wiki tactics; recorded for completeness, not re-filed individually)

1. Structure content for AI readability: answer-first format (direct answers in first 40-60 words), self-contained content units (60-180 words per single question), clear header hierarchy (one H1, H2s for sections, H3s for subsections).
2. Maximize factual density: one specific data point every 150-200 words, quantified/named statistics over vague claims, source attribution format ("According to [Source]'s [Study], [finding]"), prioritize original/proprietary data.
3. Optimize for AI crawlability: allow major AI crawlers in robots.txt (GPTBot, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot, Bytespider); fast load times (<3s); clean semantic HTML; Schema.org markup (Article, FAQPage, HowTo, Organization).
4. Build GEO citation hooks — five content patterns: Definitive Definition (quotable definition in first 200 words), Numbered Framework, Statistic Anchor (data + source attribution), Comparison Table, Expert Insight (named-expert quotations).
5. Optimize for platform-specific behaviors: ChatGPT (comprehensive guides/FAQ/clear headers), Google AI Overviews (traditional SEO foundation — 54.5% of citations from top-10 organic), Perplexity (freshness, multiple sources, tables/numbered lists).
6. Keep content fresh: quarterly audits of highest-value content, visible "last updated" dates, monitor AI citation tracking.
7. Build third-party authority: Wikipedia presence, digital PR/news coverage (20.3% of citations), Reddit engagement (8.1% of ChatGPT citations from community content), LinkedIn thought leadership.

### Performance metrics
- "AI search optimization can increase content visibility in AI-generated responses by up to 40%" (Princeton/Georgia Tech/IIT Delhi, ACM SIGKDD 2024) — this is the GEO founding paper's "Cite Sources" tactic figure, already in this wiki with fuller context (including the C-SEO Bench debunking of most such tactics under competitive retesting).
- AI-referred website sessions grew 527% year-over-year in early 2025 (Previsible, no link given)
- 50% of consumers used AI-powered search as primary information method by October 2025 (McKinsey)
- AI Overview presence grew from 30% to 48% of tracked queries, a 58% increase (BrightEdge, no link given)
- **Overlap between AI Overview citations and organic rankings grew from 32.3% to 54.5%** (BrightEdge, no link given)
- 95% of AI search citations originate from third-party sources (OtterlyAI)
- ChatGPT citation breakdown: brand websites 44.7%, news/media 25.1%, community/forums 8.1%, encyclopedic 6.3%
- Google AI Overviews: 59.8% of citations from brand websites
- 54.5% of AI Overview citations come from URLs ranking in organic top 10 (BrightEdge)
- **FAQ schema implementation showed a 350% increase in AI citations** (OtterlyAI's own experiment: 2,379 vs. 529 citations)
- ChatGPT drives 56% of all AI search referral traffic (no link given)
- **Sources cited in AI responses are 26% fresher than traditional search results** (Monash University, no link given)
- Wikipedia presence generated the second-highest citation volume in an OtterlyAI experiment

## Common mistakes to avoid

Keyword stuffing, overly robotic machine-only content, ignoring traditional SEO foundation, neglecting Wikipedia/PR/community engagement, publishing without a refresh cadence.
