## Core concept

Introduces an **"AI entity footprint audit"** — a framework for evaluating how
well AI systems understand an organization based on publicly available digital
signals. Rather than auditing individual marketing assets in isolation, the
approach examines the *collective* understanding those assets create together.

Key framing insight: "AI-powered search requires you to look beyond whether
individual assets are optimized" — the question is whether a business's
collective digital presence helps AI systems understand its identity,
capabilities, and trustworthiness.

## The six dimensions

The audit evaluates an organization across six dimensions:

1. **Identity** — Can AI consistently explain what the organization does, who
   it serves, and where it operates, across all platforms?
2. **Differentiation** — Does the business clearly articulate why customers
   should choose it over competitors, with supporting evidence?
3. **Evidence** — Are claims reinforced through independent validation
   (reviews, certifications, media coverage, testimonials)?
4. **Consistency** — Do multiple sources reinforce the same understanding, or
   do they present conflicting information?
5. **Relationships** — Are connections to industry, partners, associations,
   and communities clearly established?
6. **Specialization** — What is the business genuinely known for, based on
   recurring themes across digital touchpoints?

## Four signal categories

Signals that contribute to organizational understanding:

- **Owned signals** — website, service pages, structured data, team profiles
- **Customer signals** — reviews, testimonials, case studies
- **Third-party signals** — press mentions, awards, business directories,
  certifications
- **Ecosystem signals** — partnerships, associations, speaking engagements,
  community involvement

## Scoring system (0–5 per dimension)

- **0** — No meaningful evidence
- **1** — Very limited evidence
- **2** — Basic but fragmented evidence
- **3** — Clear foundational understanding
- **4** — Strong, consistent, well-supported
- **5** — Exceptional understanding across independent sources

## How to perform the audit

1. **Initial assessment** — Ask an AI system to describe your business:
   "Tell me everything you know about [Business Name]" — covering identity,
   specialization, differentiation, and supporting evidence. Repeat across
   multiple AI platforms.
2. **Website review** — Assess whether homepage, About page, service pages,
   and structured data clearly communicate identity and differentiation.
3. **Google Business Profile comparison** — Verify consistency between website
   and GBP descriptions; emphasize identical services and specializations.
4. **Customer analysis** — Identify recurring themes in reviews to understand
   what customers genuinely recognize about the business.
5. **Third-party validation** — Search for independent evidence: press
   coverage, podcast appearances, certifications, awards.
6. **Holistic evaluation** — Step back and determine whether the available
   evidence answers the fundamental questions about identity, differentiation,
   trustworthiness, and uniqueness.

## Implementation tool

The author created an **"AI Entity Footprint Starter Audit"** as a Custom GPT
that performs an automated first-pass analysis across the six dimensions.

## Industry variations

The framework is consistent regardless of business type, but evidence sources
vary:

- **Local businesses** — Google Business Profile, reviews, citations
- **Professional services** — expertise signals (publications, speaking
  engagements)
- **SaaS companies** — integrations, partnerships, ecosystem relationships
- **Ecommerce brands** — product reviews and comparisons
