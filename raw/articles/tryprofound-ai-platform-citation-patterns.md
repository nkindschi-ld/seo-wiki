# AI Platform Citation Patterns: How ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity Source Information

Author: Nick Lafferty, Founding Marketing Engineer (Profound)
Publish date: 2025-06-05 (updated August 2025)
Data period: August 2024 – June 2025
Dataset: 680 million citations
Source: https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns
Retrieved: 2026-07-23

## Methodology

Analyzed citation patterns across three major AI platforms (ChatGPT,
Google AI Overviews, Perplexity) by tracking which sources each
platform references in its responses over a 10-month period.

## ChatGPT citation patterns

- Leading source: Wikipedia (7.8% of total citations; 47.9% of top 10)
- Pattern: preference for authoritative knowledge bases and established
  media
- Other top sources: Reddit (1.8%), Forbes (1.1%), G2 (1.1%)
- Characteristic: heavy concentration in encyclopedia-style content

## Google AI Overviews citation patterns

- Leading sources: Reddit (2.2%) and YouTube (1.9%) of total citations
- Top 10 distribution more balanced across multiple source types
- Pattern: "balanced social-professional mix"
- Notable sources: Quora (1.5%), LinkedIn (1.3%)

## Perplexity citation patterns

- Leading source: Reddit (6.6% of total; 46.7% of top 10)
- Pattern: "community-driven information" emphasis
- Secondary sources: YouTube (2.0%), Gartner (1.0%)
- Characteristic: strong reliance on peer discussion platforms

## Domain analysis (TLD patterns, aggregate across all three platforms)

- .com domains: 80.41% of citations
- .org domains: 11.29% of citations
- Country-specific ccTLDs (.uk, .au, .br, .ca): ~3.5% combined
- Tech TLDs (.io, .ai): growing presence despite being newer

## Strategic implications

"A one-size-fits-all approach to AI visibility cannot succeed" given
platform-specific source preferences. Content strategies must align
with each platform's distinct information-sourcing philosophy rather
than assuming uniform citation behavior across AI systems.
