# 1 in 8 Social Media AI Citations Point to LinkedIn: AI Search Study by OtterlyAI

Source: https://otterly.ai/blog/linkedin-ai-search-citations-study/
Author: Rick Tousseyn
Published: June 3, 2026

---

## Methodology

- Sample size: 2,061,071 total citation records; 384,205 unique LinkedIn URLs analyzed; 161,440 unique content URLs after filtering; 1,310,455 total citations after deduplication
- AI search platforms studied: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini
- Observation period: January 1 - June 1, 2026 (5 months)
- Author attribution: identifiable for 88.5% of analyzed URLs; gender inferred from names, pronouns, and profile signals

## Key findings

### LinkedIn's overall share

- Accounts for nearly 1 in 8 social media citations in AI Search
- Monthly AI citations grew 49.9% from January to May 2026
- Share increased from 7.8% (January) to 11.7% (May)

### Content type performance

| Content Type | Share of URLs | Share of Citations | Avg Citations/URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pulse articles | 63.0% | 72.2% | 8.5 |
| Posts | 32.8% | 26.1% | 5.9 |
| Profiles | 4.1% | 1.7% | 3.0 |
| Feed updates | 0.1% | 0.1% | 3.2 |

### Author attribution impact

| Author Type | URL Share | Citation Share | Avg Citations/URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Named individuals | 87.8% | 91.7% | 8.5 |
| Company/unattributed | 12.2% | 8.3% | 5.5 |

### Platform-specific citation distribution

| Platform | Citations | Share | Pulse Article % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perplexity | 567,133 | 43.3% | 70.1% |
| Google AI Overviews | 291,299 | 22.2% | 72.1% |
| ChatGPT | 245,352 | 18.7% | 73.8% |
| Google AI Mode | 117,376 | 9.0% | 71.3% |
| Microsoft Copilot | 89,257 | 6.8% | 90.2% |
| Gemini | 38 | 0.01% | 92.1% |

### Content engagement metrics and AI citations

Near-zero Pearson correlation between citations and:
- Likes: r = -0.06
- Comments: r = -0.04
- Emojis: r = -0.02
- Hashtags: r = -0.02
- Word count alone: r = 0.03

Media presence showed no citation advantage:
- Posts with video: 5.85 avg citations
- Posts without video: 7.87 avg citations
- Posts with images: 7.14 avg citations
- Posts without images: 8.23 avg citations

### Content length comparison

| Content Type | Median Words | Average Words |
|---|---|---|
| Pulse articles | 1,021 | 1,365 |
| Posts | 185 | 234 |

### Citation concentration pattern

- Top 1% of URLs: 30.2% of all citations
- Top 5% of URLs: 54.6% of all citations
- Top 10% of URLs: 66.8% of all citations
- Top 25% of URLs: 83.3% of all citations
- 87.4% of URLs cited only once across all platforms

### Gender attribution

Among identifiable individual authors:
- Men received 76.4% of citations
- Women received 23.5% of citations
- Pattern consistent across major platforms (23-24% for women)

## Core conclusions

AI Search engines cite LinkedIn pulse articles far more than posts or profiles. Key strategic recommendations:

1. Prioritize long-form pulse articles over standard posts
2. Publish under individual names rather than company pages
3. Focus on Perplexity and Google AI Overviews (drive most LinkedIn citations)
4. Optimize for Copilot through articles (90.2% of its citations)
5. Prioritize answer quality and extractability over engagement metrics

Overarching insight: what gets a post liked does not get it cited — a disconnect between human engagement and AI reference selection.
