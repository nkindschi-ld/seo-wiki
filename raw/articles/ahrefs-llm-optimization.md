# LLMO: 10 Ways to Work Your Brand Into AI Answers

Source: https://ahrefs.com/blog/llm-optimization/
Author: Louise Linehan, Content Marketer at Ahrefs
Published/Updated: April 24, 2025

---

## Definition & overview

LLM optimization (LLMO) involves positioning your brand — positioning,
products, people, and surrounding information — for mentions, links, and
native content inclusion in language models. Distinct from AI Overview
optimization, though one can lead to the other. "Harvard Business Review
goes so far as to say that SEOs will soon be known as LLMOs."

Market context: global LLM market projected to grow 36% (2024-2030);
chatbot growth expected to reach 23% by 2030; Gartner predicts 50% of
search engine traffic gone by 2028.

## Benefits of LLMO

Future-proofs brand visibility; first-mover advantage; reduces competitor
link/citation space; integrates into personalized customer conversations;
increases brand recommendations in high-purchase-intent contexts; drives
referral traffic; improves organic search visibility by proxy.

## Two LLM types

1. **Self-contained LLMs** (e.g. Claude) — train on fixed historical
   datasets, cannot access real-time information.
2. **RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) LLMs** (e.g. Perplexity,
   Gemini) — retrieve live information, can cite sources with links, can
   send referral traffic. Perplexity reportedly refers traffic even to
   publishers who block its crawler.

## 10 LLMO strategies

1. **Invest in PR to associate brand with the right topics.** LLMs
   understand meaning via semantic proximity: words/concepts convert to
   tokens → numeric embeddings → semantic space, with cosine similarity
   determining relationships. Example: Claude recommends Herman Miller,
   Steelcase Gesture, HAG Capisco for "ergonomic chairs" due to semantic
   proximity to "improving posture." Herman Miller generated 273 pages of
   "ergonomic" press mentions via reviews, press releases, product-led PR,
   paid affiliate programs, and sponsored content. Implementation: track
   share of voice for key topics, monitor mentions/links, test LLMs with
   focus-topic questions, gap-analyze against competitors.

2. **Include quotes and statistics in content.** Study of 10,000
   real-world queries across search-augmented LLMs (Bing, Google) tested
   optimization techniques (visibility uplift / relevance score):
   Quotes 27.2/24.7, Statistics 25.2/23.7, Fluency 24.7/21.9, Citations
   24.6/21.9, Technical terms 22.7/21.4, Easy-to-understand 22.0/20.5,
   Authoritative tone 21.3/22.9, Unique words 20.5/20.4, No optimization
   19.3/19.3, Keyword stuffing 17.7/20.2. Quotes/statistics/citations
   showed 30-40% visibility uplift — they reinforce authority/credibility,
   generate links, and are routinely referenced in LLM training corpora.

3. **Conduct entity research, not keyword research.** Focus on how LLMs
   perceive brand relationships. Google's "3 Pillars of Ranking" (per
   Bernard Huang): body text (what the document says about itself),
   anchor text (what the web says about it), user interactions (what
   users say about it). Entity identification: on-page (NLP finds
   topics/page embeddings) and site-level (site embeddings across the
   whole site, site focus score — concentration on a topic, site radius —
   variation between page topics and overall site topic). Tools: Google's
   Natural Language API (paid), Inlinks' Entity Analyzer (free,
   site-level), Ahrefs' AI Content Helper (uncovered entities +
   suggestions). Process: audit existing brand entities, find the
   disconnect between current LLM perception and desired positioning,
   create content to build new associations.

4. **Use Ahrefs Brand Radar** to track brand mentions in AI Overviews and
   benchmark against competitors: search topics, discover mention
   frequency, benchmark competitors, track share of voice. Workflow:
   identify competitors with the greatest AI Overview visibility,
   reverse-engineer their strategy, test PR impact on AI visibility,
   find third-party sites with strong visibility for partnerships.

5. **Claim Wikipedia listings.** Every LLM trains on Wikipedia, often as
   its largest data source. Four requirements: (1) **Notability** —
   recognized as an independent entity via news/books/academic
   papers/interviews; (2) **Verifiability** — claims backed by reliable
   third-party sources; (3) **Neutral point of view** — unbiased, neutral
   tone; (4) **Avoiding conflict of interest** — written by
   brand-impartial contributors, facts over promotion. Build edit history
   and credibility before claiming a listing. Secondary benefit:
   Wikipedia presence increases likelihood of appearing in Google's
   Knowledge Graph. Tool: Carl Hendy's Google Knowledge Graph Search Tool.

6. **Research brand questions to optimize for LLM prompts.** Use search
   volume data (Ahrefs: search topic → Questions tab → toggle Brand
   filter) to find brand questions that might appear in LLM conversations
   (e.g. "What is ahrefs?", "How to use ahrefs?"). Also type "Is [brand
   name]..." into ChatGPT/Perplexity and observe autocomplete — these
   differ from Google autocomplete/PAA, offering distinct targets.

7. **Invest in user-generated content on Reddit.** Reddit's own S-1 SEC
   filing: "Our content is particularly important for AI – it is
   foundational to how leading LLMs have been trained." Strategies
   without spam: community building, AMAs, influencer partnerships,
   encouraging brand-based user content. Track growth via Ahrefs' Top
   Pages report on the Reddit domain, filtered by brand keyword.

8. **Provide LLM feedback.** Gemini reportedly doesn't train on user
   prompts/responses unless explicitly shared for improvement — rating
   responses (especially on live/retrieval-based LLMs: Gemini,
   Perplexity, Copilot) helps correct inaccurate brand information.
   Example: a brand ("Site of Sites") was eventually recognized by
   Gemini through response rating/feedback.

9. **Don't neglect everyday SEO.** Correction noted in-article: AI
   crawlers can only access HTML, not client-side-rendered content or
   schema — so schema markup itself isn't a direct LLM-visibility lever.
   Seer Interactive study: 10,000 high-search-volume, purchase-intent
   questions (finance + SaaS sectors), GPT-4o API measuring brand-mention
   frequency, correlated with Google/Bing SERP data. Findings: organic
   rankings drive LLM mentions (~0.65 correlation strength); backlinks
   have a surprisingly neutral impact. Correlation grew stronger when
   filtering to solution-focused sites (excluding forums/social/
   aggregators). Implication: SEO remains crucial for LLM visibility.

10. **Hack your way in (black-hat warning).** Harvard study, "Manipulating
    Large Language Models to Increase Product Visibility": (a)
    **Strategic Text Sequencing (STS)** — originally designed to bypass
    LLM safety guardrails, repurposed via trial-and-error character
    optimization to manipulate brand/product recommendations; ~40% of
    evaluations showed higher target-product ranking. (b) **Preference
    manipulation attacks** — crafted website content or plugin docs with
    prompt injections trick LLMs into promoting the attacker's product
    and discrediting competitors; example: a fake camera product page
    raised its recommendation rate from 34% to 59.4%, and biased content
    increased selection 2.5x over legitimate brands. Real-world report of
    a marketer experiencing AI-based brand sabotage from a competitor
    using false information. Warning: black-hat opportunists will exploit
    LLM vulnerabilities as in early SEO days; brands need
    reputation-preservation strategies alongside optimization.

## Additional insights

Brand entity recognition reportedly takes ~30 encounters through
different channels (Amanda King) — LLM optimization may need more.
Search Experience Optimization (SXO) — considering the full customer
journey — aligns with LLMO. Once LLM mentions increase, strategically
link from frequently-cited gateway pages to funnel value through the
site. Core philosophy: LLMO is considered, consistent brand building with
less direct control over the discovery pathway than classic SEO offers.
