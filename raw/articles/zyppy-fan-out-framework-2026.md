# Fan-out Framework: 5 Steps to Optimize for Fan-out Queries

Author: Cyrus Shepard
Publication Date: 2026-06-25
Source: https://signal.zyppy.com/p/fan-out-framework (Zyppy Signal with Cyrus Shepard, Substack)

## Article Overview

This guide presents a systematic approach to optimizing content for AI fan-out queries — specific sub-queries that AI engines generate to synthesize answers from multiple sources.

## Key Framework: 5-Step Process

1. Identify a ranking keyword from your existing top 10-20 Google results
2. Discover fan-out queries using specialized tools
3. Consolidate important topics from fan-out data
4. Optimize existing or create new pages targeting those queries
5. Measure results through webmaster tools and AI trackers

## Key Principles

On Fan-out Variability:
"Fan-out queries, like AI, are probabilistic and personalized, and they vary greatly across AI models and even within user sessions."

Content Quality Warning:
The author advises against creating numerous low-quality pages covering every fan-out topic, noting Google increasingly demotes scaled content.

Optimization Speed:
AI answers can respond to content updates faster than traditional search because they "use 70 days of user data rather than the 13 months used for traditional search results."
