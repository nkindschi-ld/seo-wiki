## Methodology

- **1,094 US categories**, tracked monthly in ChatGPT from **January
  through June 2026**.
- Each category is a **topical cluster of five representative prompts**.
  "Think of a category as a topic like 'payroll software'... and the
  five prompts as the standard questions someone would ask about each"
  (definition, comparison, alternatives, use case, purchasing decision).
- Dataset covers **220,000+ domains, 50,000+ brands, 600,000+ citations,
  220,000+ URLs**.

## Ownership definitions (precise)

- **Category owner:** "the brand with the highest share of mentions,
  named in at least four of five prompts, with at least a
  five-percentage-point lead over the runner-up."
- **Emerging leader:** "the most-mentioned brand appears in at least
  three prompts but does not meet the owner threshold."
- **Unsettled category:** "no brand appears in at least three prompts."

## Topic ownership distribution

- **15.2%** of categories have a clear owner.
- **31.2%** have an emerging leader.
- **53.7%** are unsettled (no dominant brand).

## Inverse-demand paradox

"Higher search volume usually means stronger competition. In ChatGPT,
it's the opposite." Only **11.3%** of high-demand topics had clear
owners vs. **19%** in lower-demand categories.

## SEO metrics are weak predictors of topic ownership

Comparing owners vs. runners-up (directional win-rate across pairs, NOT
a correlation coefficient):

- Owners had higher **branded search volume** in **55.7%** of pairs.
- Owners had higher **organic traffic** in **48.4%** of pairs.
- Owners had a higher **Authority Score** in **52.5%** of pairs.

"Only branded search volume was a statistically significant
differentiator, and even then, the edge was modest." No coefficient
details provided.

## Leadership stability

- Clear category owners "stayed on top in **90.4%** of month-over-month
  comparisons."
- Topics where the leader held: **median lead of 2.9 percentage points**.
- Topics where the leader flipped: **median lead of 1.3 percentage
  points**.

## Mentions vs. citations

- The **74%** figure derives from "Kevin's earlier Growth Memo research"
  (a separate, external study): "74% of users in that research chose the
  top-mentioned brand as their final pick."
- For THIS study: "only **21%** of the most-cited domains in a category
  were also the most-mentioned brand, and the two correlate slightly
  negatively (**-0.229**)."

## Practitioner recommendations

1. Identify topical areas matching customer concerns and competitive
   landscape.
2. Analyze current topical authority using Semrush's toolkit.
3. Focus efforts on **2-3 relevant categories with existing signal**.
4. Build topical authority through: "broad content coverage... relevant
   mentions in the sources ChatGPT draws from... and strong entity
   signals."
