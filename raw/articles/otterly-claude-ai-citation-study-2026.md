# Claude AI Citations Study: How to Get Cited in 2026

**Author:** Rick Tousseyn
**Publish date:** 2026-06-30
**Source:** Otterly.ai Blog
**URL:** https://otterly.ai/blog/claude-ai-citation-study/

## TL;DR

1. Brand sites dominate Claude's citations: 64.0% of all citations come from first-party brand content.
2. The top 10 domains represent just 9.5% of all citations — Claude's citations are highly distributed.
3. Social media is negligible for Claude (0.9% of citations). Reddit was cited 0 times.
4. LinkedIn accounts for 43.1% of Claude's social media citations (the only platform that matters).
5. 32.9% of cited domains appear only once across the entire dataset.

## Citation Distribution (Source Category Breakdown)

| Category | Share | # Domains |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | 64.0% | 10,842 domains |
| News/Media | 14.9% | 1,688 domains |
| Blogs/Personal | 5.4% | 1,041 domains |
| Education | 2.7% | 518 domains |
| Social Media | 0.9% | 37 domains |

## Social Media Breakdown (within 0.9%)

- LinkedIn: 43.1% of social media citations
- Reddit: 0 citations
- Twitter/X: Minimal/negligible
- Other platforms: Negligible

## Key Metrics

- Total citations analyzed: 379,321
- Unique domains cited: 16,406
- Citation distribution: highly dispersed (top 10 = 9.5%, median domain cited ~1-2 times)
- Domains cited once: 32.9%
- Data period: June 2026
- Query types: SaaS and technology

## Methodology

Analyzed 379,321 Claude citations across 16,406 domains in 11 source categories during June 2026. Tracked which domains Claude cited, their classification, and citation frequency. Data focused on SaaS and technology queries.

## Main Takeaways (source's framing)

1. First-party brand content dominates, but remains widely distributed across thousands of sources.
2. Social media is negligible for Claude visibility; Reddit and Twitter are effectively absent.
3. Well-structured, reference-grade content drives citations more than popularity metrics.
4. Citation patterns remained stable throughout June, enabling reliable planning.
