# How To Integrate GEO With SEO

Source: https://searchengineland.com/integrate-geo-seo-453351
Author: Christina Adame
Publish Date: March 19, 2025

---

## Core definitions

**GEO**: optimizing entities — brands, products, concepts, people, ideas
— to appear in AI-generated responses (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews,
Gemini, Perplexity). "SEO and GEO are partners, each playing distinct
yet interconnected roles." SEO provides foundational discoverability;
GEO extends brand recognition across the datasets/authoritative sources
that shape AI's training/retrieval data.

## How AI search differs from traditional search

Traditional search: crawlability, indexability, rankability.
AI-driven search adds a fourth stage: **retrievability** — "how
effectively AI can access, interpret, and prioritize information about
your brand when forming responses." Rankings alone don't guarantee AI
visibility because LLMs don't rank like traditional engines — they build
responses dynamically from contextual patterns/entity connections, not a
ranked index lookup.

**Authority shift**: from backlinks (traditional SEO's authority signal)
to contextual relevance, entity associations, and consistent brand
mentions in authoritative sources.

## How LLMs learn and generate responses

LLMs ingest unstructured training data and learn patterns from word
co-occurrence/contextual frequency; at query time they predict
plausible next words from entity understanding, not database lookup.
Example: Nike queries surface "innovation," "sportswear," "branding"
because those words co-occur with Nike frequently in training data.

**RAG** (Retrieval-Augmented Generation): advanced LLMs combine
foundational training knowledge with real-time retrieval, prioritizing
timely/authoritative news sites, reputable industry publications,
established knowledge platforms, discussion forums, and knowledge
graphs.

## Retrievability framework: three pillars

- **Presence** — consistent brand mentions in contexts that shape AI's
  training data and retrieval sources.
- **Recognition** — credibility built through consistent, contextually
  relevant mentions and clear associations with trusted entities.
- **Accessibility** — brand information structured (on-site and across
  the web) for easy AI retrieval and prioritization.

## Integration strategy: on-page SEO

- Build topical authority with entity-rich content: pinpoint core
  topics/entities, structure into pillar+cluster content, fill content
  gaps, reference widely-known entities/experts.
- Reinforce entity relationships via internal linking (AI association
  strengthening) and external linking/citation to AI-recognized
  authoritative sources.
- Create contextually relevant content aligned with evolving intent:
  regularly review AI-generated responses about core topics, close gaps
  against current AI understanding, optimize for long-tail
  question-based queries.
- Establish thought leadership: original research/data-backed reports,
  cited sources, clearly attributed expert quotes.
- Structure content for AI processing: FAQs, bullet points, step-by-step
  instructions, tables; concise natural language; simple sentence
  structure for brand-entity statements.
- Keep content timely: track Reddit/Quora/social/niche-forum discussions,
  update content for trending topics, prioritize freshness for queries
  that trigger hybrid LLM search.

## Integration strategy: off-page SEO

- Identify and target AI-trusted sources (major industry sites, trusted
  niche publications, authoritative forums) and actively participate.
- Use digital PR to influence AI-shaping conversations: identify
  publications AI consistently references per topic, build
  journalist/blogger relationships, produce citation-worthy original
  research.
- Maintain consistent brand representation/messaging across all
  channels; reinforce repeated associations with key entities/topics.
- Strengthen Knowledge Graph presence: Wikipedia notability, precise
  Wikidata entries, actively managed Google Business Profile.
- Proactively engage in topical conversations on Reddit/Quora/niche
  forums/social, positioning the brand at the center of emerging
  trends.
- Continuously track/learn/adapt: monitor AI-generated brand mentions,
  refine off-page approach, adapt quickly to shifts in AI's brand
  perception.

## Integration strategy: technical SEO

- Keep data simple/clear: straightforward HTML, limit
  important-content-blocking JavaScript, clear descriptive metadata,
  semantic content organization.
- Use structured data for entity recognition: Organization/Person/
  Product/Article schema, clearly defined brand/concept markup.
- Prioritize site speed/performance: image/script optimization, CDNs,
  Core Web Vitals, browser caching.
- Help AI crawl content: explicitly allow AI crawlers (GPTBot,
  PerplexityBot) via robots.txt, fix broken links/blocked resources, use
  structured XML sitemaps.
- Optimize media for multimodal visibility: descriptive filenames/alt
  text, OpenGraph tags, accurate video transcripts/captions.

## Measurement framework

Traditional SEO KPIs (rankings, CTR, traffic) don't fully capture
zero-click AI interactions. Per Rand Fishkin, "over 44% of Google
searches are for branded terms" — AI reshapes rather than replaces
search habits.

New metrics to track:
- **Beyond rankings**: impressions/visibility in AI Overviews, knowledge
  panels, discussions, featured snippets.
- **Branded engagement**: branded search volume, direct traffic,
  returning visitors — rising branded search signals successful
  AI-driven discovery.
- **Business outcomes**: leads, conversions, revenue stability despite
  possible organic-traffic decline.
- **AI referral traffic**: GA4 regex filter to capture visits from
  ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity/etc:
  `(.*gpt.*|.*chatgpt.*|.*openai.*|.*neeva.*|.*writesonic.*|.*nimble.*|.*outrider.*|.*perplexity.*|.*google.*bard.*|.*bard.*|.*edgeservices.*|.*gemini.*google.*|.*copilot.*)`
- **AI citations/mentions**: regularly check brand appearance in
  AI-generated responses; automate via Google Sheets + ChatGPT API
  workflows.

## Overarching framework

Quotes Crystal Carter (Wix Studio, head of SEO communications): "As
SEOs, we do not need to abandon the tactics we've always relied on, but
we do need to evolve them. Where in the past we looked at crawlability,
indexability, and stopped at rankability, we now need to add in
retrievability." Frames GEO/SEO integration as requiring cross-team
collaboration (owned media, earned media, digital PR, branding, social,
creative) — "GEO is a team sport."
