# These SEO Strategies Drive 90% of Your AI Visibility

Author: Cyrus Shepard
Publication Date: 2026-08-04
Publication: Zyppy Signal (Substack)
Source: https://signal.zyppy.com/p/seo-strategies-for-ai-search

## Core Thesis

Traditional SEO practices effectively support AI visibility. The author argues that most businesses can achieve 90%+ of AI visibility goals through adapted SEO fundamentals and tweaked tactics targeting specific AI search mechanisms, rather than pursuing separate GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) strategies.

## Three Main Sections & Frameworks

### 1. Make Your Content AI Eligible

Key Claims:
- AI systems require crawlability and understanding of website content
- Many organizations treat indexing as "solved" despite emerging complexities

AI-Specific Complexities Identified:
- Multiple user agents with varying rules appearing frequently
- CDN and Cloudflare bot control rules
- Snippet control directives (`data-nosnippet`)
- Common Crawl indexing separate from Google
- JavaScript rendering gaps in AI crawlers

SEO Levers:
- Audit robots.txt aggressively for AI bot access
- Distinguish between training bots (GPTBot, Google-Extended) and search bots (OAI-SearchBot)
- Implement consistent bot control policies across CDN/firewall
- Verify text visibility without heavy JavaScript rendering
- Check for noindex, nosnippet, max-snippet directives

Tools Mentioned:
- AI Difference Engine extension (Gray Dot Co)

### 2. Win AI Citations

Key Claims:
- AI answers frequently include citation links to external websites
- Citations correlate with increased organic and paid clicks
- Ranking highly for main terms alone isn't sufficient

AI-Specific Mechanisms:
- Fan-out queries (background subqueries performed by AI systems)
- Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) correlates high organic rankings across fan-outs with increased citations
- AI systems match content to pre-formed answers
- AI engines prioritize authoritative sites

SEO Levers:
- Rank for query fan-outs related to existing keywords
- Leverage People Also Ask questions to identify fan-outs
- Optimize existing pages rather than creating new ones
- Include evidence-backed claims and specific data
- Match formatting to question structure

Tools Mentioned:
- AlsoAsked.com

### 3. Get Recommended by AI Answers

Key Claims:
- Recommendations require "distributed consensus" from multiple trusted sources
- Emphasis shifting from keyword-rich anchor text to authentic brand mentions

AI-Specific Mechanisms:
- Topically relevant sites with genuine expertise carry more weight
- User-generated content (Reddit, LinkedIn) has outsized influence
- Third-party reviews influence initial recommendations
- Internal brand positioning affects recommendations

SEO Levers:
- Establish consistent brand positioning: [Brand] is a [product] for [audience], especially [use case], because [differentiator]
- Maintain consistent brand facts across owned properties
- Build profiles on third-party platforms (subreddits, directories, review sites, marketplaces)
- Encourage detailed, authentic customer reviews
- Pursue digital PR for authentic brand mentions

## Additional Data Points & Claims

- "Good SEO is good GEO" (attributed to Google)
- AI crawlers don't always respect robots.txt
- Existing research shows AI citations can boost traditional organic and paid clicks
- Publishers are increasingly pushing back against unlimited AI bot access

## Noted Counterpoint

Shahzad Abbas argues that publishers require protections against unlimited AI bot access, suggesting more access isn't universally optimal.

## Methodology

Research-based analysis examining how AI engines generate recommendations and cite sources, conducted over several months.
