# AI Crawlers & Bots Directory — StackFox

Source: https://stackfox.co/bots
Retrieved: 2026-07-23

## Overview

StackFox maintains a living directory of known AI bots/crawlers,
documenting which organizations operate them and (per-bot, behind
expandable UI not captured by automated fetch) their robots.txt
compliance status. Description: "All known AI crawlers and bots
tracked. See what each does, who operates it, and whether it respects
robots.txt."

## Limitation of this ingest

Individual bot names, purposes (training/indexing/retrieval), and
per-bot robots.txt-compliance status are shown behind expandable
accordion sections that automated fetching could not extract — only
the organization-level bot counts above were recoverable. Anyone
needing the exact user-agent strings for organizations not yet in the
wiki's [[robots-txt-strategy]] table (Meta, Amazon, xAI, ByteDance,
Cohere, Mistral AI, DeepSeek, Allen Institute for AI, Zhipu AI, Brave,
etc.) should visit the page directly and expand each section, or check
each vendor's own crawler documentation.
