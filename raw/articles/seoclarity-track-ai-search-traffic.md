# How to Track AI Search Traffic to Your Site in GA4

Author: Mark Traphagen
Publish date: 2025-03-25
Source: https://www.seoclarity.net/blog/track-ai-search-traffic
Retrieved: 2026-07-23

## Why tracking matters

Referral traffic from AI search engines has skyrocketed, with some
industries experiencing over a 700% spike. Many SEO professionals lack
visibility into traffic from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini,
limiting their ability to measure AI's impact on their strategy.

## Five-step GA4 implementation process

1. Create a new Traffic Acquisition report: Reports > Library > "Create
   Detail Report."
2. Add a Session Source/Medium column to view traffic source details.
3. Customize and set as default: drag the column into priority
   position.
4. Apply a filter using "Matches Partial Regex," entering the AI search
   engines separated by the "|" symbol to isolate AI-engine sources.
5. Save the report under Reports > Library for repeated access.

(The article names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini as the AI
sources to track, but does not give exact domain/source-medium values
or a full regex string.)

Search Visibility" report — part of seoClarity's "Clarity ArcAI"
platform — which measures how a brand (and competitors) appear in
results across AI search engines. It:

- Measures brand presence in AI-powered search results for key topics.
- Benchmarks visibility against competitors.
- Surfaces content gaps where AI isn't surfacing the brand.
- Illustrated with an example tracking mentions for "black formal
