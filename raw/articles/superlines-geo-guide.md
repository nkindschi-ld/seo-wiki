## Core definition

GEO is "the evolution of SEO for AI search," focused on earning
visibility within LLM responses (ChatGPT, Gemini, Mistral, Perplexity,
Copilot, Google AI Mode) rather than traditional rankings. "GEO extends
SEO, it does not replace it."

## GEO vs. traditional SEO

| Dimension | SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Optimization target | Search rankings | Citations in AI answers |
| Authority model | Backlinks/domain authority | Citation authority/structured data |
| User intent | Keyword phrases | Conversational queries |
| Success metric | CTR/traffic | Visibility score/citation frequency |
| Goal | List page in results | Become the cited answer |

**Claim**: LLMs cite only 2-7 domains per response vs. Google's 10 blue
links — visibility is more competitive.

## Three-layer AI search model

1. **Training data (slowest)**: LLM pre-training/periodic fine-tuning.
   Bot visits ~every other day per "Superlines research." Months to base
   model inclusion. Action: build durable authority, consistent facts
   across owned/third-party sources.
2. **High-volume AI search (speed depends on SEO)**: uses existing
   search indexes (Google, Bing); powers free ChatGPT, AI Overviews.
   Speed correlates with SEO strength. Action: solid SEO, structure for
   query variations, refresh regularly.
3. **Agentic AI (fastest/real-time)**: Perplexity Pro, ChatGPT Research
   Assistant, Claude Desktop with MCPs; near-real-time scraping. Action:
   agent-friendly pages (clean HTML/schema), monitor bot traffic.

## The 10-step GEO framework

1. Align GEO objectives with business KPIs (map to leads/revenue/
   signups; add "how did you hear about us" intake questions; baseline
   first).
2. Audit current AI visibility/sentiment (benchmark visibility score,
   share of voice, sentiment).
3. Map real-user prompts across the funnel (sales calls, customer
   conversations, Reddit listening; stable "prompt packs" tracked
   weekly). Example categories: "Best X for Y," "X vs Y," "Alternatives
   to X," "What is X," "Pricing for X."
3.5. Use generative AI to identify intent-rich topics: cluster real
   prompts via LLM, extract dominant entities, cross-reference citation
   frequency, prioritize high-intent commercial clusters, re-run
   monthly.
4. Structure content for AI-friendly summaries: TL;DR blocks, bullet
   lists/tables, FAQPage/HowTo schema, comparative articles (Best X, A
   vs B).
5. Optimize technical signals/schema: HTTPS everywhere, mobile speed
   under 1.8s, complete structured data, AI bot traffic tracking, add an
   `llms.txt` file. "Treat GEO like an API design problem — AI crawlers
   parse HTML, build embeddings, and extract facts the same way an API
   client consumes JSON."
6. Elevate citation authority via thought leadership: original research/
   whitepapers, data-driven commentary, target ≥20 high-authority-domain
   citations per quarter.
7. Strengthen E-E-A-T/brand trust: detailed author bios, transparent
   sourcing, review schema, audit outdated low-trust content, third-
   party reviews/certifications/contact info.
8. Integrate multimedia/data assets: charts, infographics, video
   transcripts, descriptive captions/alt text — note most AI crawlers
   can't parse JavaScript.
9. Scale prompt testing: iterative prompt library, 20-30 unique prompts
   per core topic, run daily.
10. Benchmark/report/iterate every four months: consistent cadence,
    track visibility/citation authority/sentiment, share results
    internally.

## GEO metrics/KPI matrix (vendor-proposed, benchmarks undisclosed methodology)

| Metric | Definition | Measurement | Starter benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citation Frequency | How often AI cites your domain | Track across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/Mistral/Copilot | +10-20% monthly growth for priority prompts |
| AI Brand Visibility | % of answers mentioning your brand | Mentions ÷ total answers × 100 | 15-30% in owned topics after 60-90 days |
| Share of Voice (AI SOV) | Your brand's share vs. competitors | Your mentions ÷ total competitor mentions | Top 3 within core clusters |
| Context Accuracy | Whether AI summaries describe you correctly | Manual review + sentiment/fact checks | >95% accurate on top pages |
| Prompt Coverage | % of tracked prompts where you appear | Appearing prompts ÷ total tracked | 50-70% in priority clusters |
| Assisted Conversions | Revenue influenced by AI exposure | Correlate citation spikes with branded search/pipeline | Show lift vs. baseline |

## How AI engines evaluate content (RAG)

RAG augments generation with externally retrieved documents, prioritizing
relevance, recency, trust. ChatGPT/Perplexity/Mistral reportedly "conduct
web searches for most questions" rather than relying solely on training
data. Critical citation signals: E-E-A-T, transparent author bios,
reputable citations, consistent content updates, clean semantic HTML,
valid JSON-LD schema, consistent entity tagging.

## Risk of not adopting GEO

Lost share of voice as competitors dominate AI answers; brand
misrepresentation via hallucination/outdated info; competitors defining
brand narrative in AI results; revenue loss from an untapped channel.

## FAQ

- Can GEO hurt traditional SEO? No — schema/authoritative content
  strengthens both.
- Which engines first? ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity first; Claude
  and Copilot as resources allow.
- Refresh cadence? Every 90 days.
- Who owns GEO strategy? Typically Director of Digital Marketing/Head of
  SEO, partnering with content/analytics teams.
