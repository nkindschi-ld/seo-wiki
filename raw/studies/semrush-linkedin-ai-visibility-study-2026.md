## Methodology

- **325,000 unique prompts** across three AI search tools.
- **89,000 unique LinkedIn URLs** cited in AI responses.
- **12 major industry categories**, professional/business topics.
- **Platforms:** ChatGPT Search, Google AI Mode, Perplexity.
- **Time period:** January–February 2026.
- **Metrics:** citation frequency/position, content type, author signals
  (followers, posting frequency), engagement (reactions, comments), content
  characteristics (length, media, originality), semantic similarity (0–1 scale).

## Five key takeaways

1. **LinkedIn ranks #2 in citations** across AI models — ~11% of responses on
   average.
2. **High semantic similarity (0.57–0.60)** — AI responses mirror LinkedIn
   content meaning more closely than Reddit (0.53–0.54) or Quora (0.435).
3. **Original long-form dominates:** 500–2,000-word articles and 50–299-word
   posts cited most; **95% of citations are original content.**
4. **Educational content wins:** 54–64% of cited posts share knowledge/practical
   advice; reshares only 5% of citations.
5. **Consistency beats virality:** most cited posts have 15–25 reactions; 75% of
   cited authors post frequently (5+ in four weeks).

## Findings & statistics

**Citation rates by platform (% of responses citing LinkedIn):**
- ChatGPT Search: 14.3%
- Google AI Mode: 13.5%
- Perplexity: 5.3%

**Content type distribution:**
- Articles: 50–66% of citations
- Feed posts: 15–28%
- Other formats: remainder

**Author profiles:**
- ~75% of cited authors are frequent posters (5+ posts monthly).
- ~50% have 2,000+ followers.
- Authors with <500 followers are cited **equally or more** than those with 500+.

**Content intent:**
- Knowledge/advice-driven: 54–64% (Google AI Mode ~66%).
- Product/service promotion: secondary share.

**Company vs. individual citations (by platform):**
- Perplexity: **59% Company Pages**
- ChatGPT Search: **59% individual creators**
- Google AI Mode: **59% individual creators**

**Engagement patterns:**
- Median cited posts: 15–25 reactions, <1 comment.
- High virality not required for AI visibility.

## Recommendations

- Clearly define key concepts and brand-related terms in your content.
- Structure articles: headline, direct answer early, logical flow.
- Target 500–2,000 words for articles; 50–299 for posts.
- Establish regular publishing schedules to maximize citation opportunities.
- Build employee-advocacy programs; enable SMEs to post consistently.
- Answer high-intent customer questions directly.
- Balance Company Page content with individual creator posts.
- Prioritize relevance/expertise over engagement metrics or follower counts.
