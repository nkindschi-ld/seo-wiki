## Methodology

- **175 brands** tested across **five verticals**: legal, healthcare,
  SaaS, financial services, and ecommerce/retail.
- **140 brands** evaluated for recognition analysis; **150 brands**
  formed the mention-rate cohort.
- **8 platforms**: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Google
  AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Meta AI.
- **Recognition testing:** asked each platform to describe brands; graded
  responses as correct, vague, outdated, wrong, or not recognized.
- **Mention testing:** measured brand appearances in AI responses to
  standardized **category research prompts** and separate **problem
  awareness prompts**.
- **Expanded analysis:** cross-referenced with organic rankings, organic
  traffic, third-party web mentions, and Knowledge Graph presence.

## Recognition vs. mention gap (headline finding)

- "96 percent of the brands we tested were described accurately"
- "89 percent of the brands we measured never appeared in AI-generated
  answers to category research questions"

Being *recognized* (the model can describe you) is nearly universal;
being *mentioned* in an answer to a buying-stage question is rare.

## Platform-specific recognition rates

- Google AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Copilot: exceeded
  **83%** accuracy across all verticals.
- Perplexity: recognized **fewer than 55%** of SaaS and ecommerce brands.
- Meta AI: recognized only **46%** of SaaS brands.

## What predicts AI mentions (correlations)

- Referring domains: **0.49**
- Third-party web mentions: **0.45**

**Threshold:** "Brands with fewer than 2,000 indexed web pages mentioning
them were named in AI answers just 3 percent of the time."

## Citation behavior across the buyer journey

**Category research prompts:**
- "99.99 percent of the 49,391 citations we analyzed pointed to
  third-party websites rather than the brand's own domain."
- Only **4 of 150 brands** earned citations to their own website.

**Problem awareness prompts:**
- Brands were named in just **0.10%** of answers despite their
  educational content being cited.

**Consideration-stage lift:** brands "were named more than twelve times
as often" in category-research answers than in early-stage
problem-awareness answers.

## Recommendations (stage-specific)

1. **Early journey:** publish educational content addressing
   problem-awareness questions — AI relies on this content to build
   answers while typically excluding brand names.
2. **Consideration stage:** build presence on directories and comparison
   sites where AI conducts category research (12x more brand mentions
   there than early-stage prompts).
3. **Industry-specific off-site strategy:** legal-services citations
   concentrate in prestige directories, while SaaS citations scatter
   across 10,000+ unique domains — tailor by vertical.
