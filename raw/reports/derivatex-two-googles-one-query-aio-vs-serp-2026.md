# Two Googles, One Query — Google AIO vs. SERP Source Overlap Benchmark

- **Publisher:** DerivateX Research
- **Author:** Apoorv Sharma
- **Published:** 2026-07-06 (5th in a series on AI-engine citation behavior)
- **URL:** https://derivatex.agency/report/google-aio-vs-serp-benchmark/
- **Archived:** 2026-08-03 (structured extract via fetch; agency report stored for
  internal wiki reference)

Full report title: "Two Googles, One Query: Google AI Overviews share only 35% of
their sources with the search results below them."

## Methodology (fully disclosed)

- 100 buyer-intent queries across 20 B2B software categories (5 queries/category)
- 1,259 AI Overview (AIO) citations analyzed; 1,000 traditional SERP results captured
- 445 AIO-recommended products matched against SERP domains/titles
- Both AIO panel and top-10 organic captured simultaneously in single search sessions
- Same-domain presence = "overlap"; products listed only inside listicles counted
  conservatively (not counted as overlap)
- Conducted June–July 2026
- **Limitations acknowledged:** 100 B2B-software queries only (absolute % won't hold
  outside this vertical); AIO non-deterministic (geo/personalization/time shift
  results); single-session captures exclude run-to-run variance; product overlap
  measured conservatively (true overlap slightly higher); category averages from 5
  queries each — directional, not definitive.

## Core thesis

Google presents two distinct discovery layers on one screen — the AIO panel and the
classic SERP — operating as separate content ecosystems with divergent source
preferences. Ranking highly on the classic SERP no longer guarantees AIO citation.

## Key findings

**1. Source divergence.** Only **35%** of URLs cited in the AIO also appear in
Google's top-10 organic; **65% of AIO citations are AIO-exclusive** (don't rank in
top-10). Asymmetric: only **42%** of Google's top-10 domains get cited by the AIO
above them.

**2. Product-recommendation split.** Only **28%** of AIO-recommended products also
rank in Google's SERP for the same query; **72% AIO-exclusive**. Range: 0% overlap
(32 of 100 queries) to 100% overlap (5 of 100).

**3. Overlap quality (when shared).** **72% of shared sources rank in Google's
top-5**; median position **#4**. Distribution: 59 at #1, 50 at #2, 43 at #3, 47 at
#4; only 2 instances from #9–10. Implication: ranking well concentrates presence in
the shared 35% corpus; page-2 rankings rarely cross over.

**4. YouTube–Reddit inversion (largest structural gap).**
- YouTube: AIO cites it in 51/100 queries vs. SERP 7/100 → AIO uses YouTube **7.3×**
  more. "The AI Overview treats video as a first-class source" for product contexts.
- Reddit: SERP ranks it in 82/100 queries vs. AIO 43/100 → SERP ranks Reddit **1.9×**
  more often than AIO. (Opposite of ChatGPT, which favors Reddit.)

**5. Source-type mix (AIO vs SERP share):**

| Source type | AIO | SERP | Delta (AIO) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Third-party listicles | 63.4% | 55.4% | +7.9 pts |
| Vendor own-site | 16.8% | 20.2% | -3.4 pts |
| YouTube/video | 9.0% | 2.3% | +6.6 pts |
| Review sites (G2, Gartner) | 5.0% | 9.9% | -4.9 pts |
| Reddit/forums | 4.7% | 11.0% | -6.3 pts |
| News/trade press | 1.2% | 1.1% | ~same |

**6. Category-level divergence.** Highest divergence (20–27% overlap): help desk 20%,
project management 20%, video hosting 23%, accounting 24%, contract management 27%.
Moderate (30–39%): marketing automation, app monitoring, spend management, ITSM,
social media management, CRM. Partial convergence (40–49%): HR 40%, compliance
automation 46%, tech pack 47%, AI time tracking 49%. Exception: **QuickBooks hosting
62%** — the only category strongly tracking Google rankings. Pattern: mature
third-party-listicle ecosystems diverge most; thin-listicle categories converge on
Google.

## Inferential claims (no direct CTR/traffic data)

- Ranking #1 on Google is "no longer sufficient" for AIO citation but remains
  "necessary" for the shared visibility (72% of overlaps are top-5).
- Buyer path bifurcates: whichever surface the user clicks first determines the
  shortlist they carry forward.
- Companion case study (Gumlet) cited re: "ChatGPT mentions into 20% of inbound
  revenue," but AIO traffic impact not directly quantified.

## Key quotes

- "Ranking #1 on Google no longer means being cited by Google's AI Overview."
- "65% of what Google's AI Overview cites does not rank in Google's own top-10."
- "The AI Overview reads a video-heavy corpus that Google itself does not surface."
- "The AI Overview is a separate discovery layer with a separate content preference."

## Stated strategic implications

1. YouTube as first-class AIO surface (walkthroughs, category explainers, comparison
   videos).
2. Third-party listicle placement is a prerequisite for AIO recommendation (63.4% of
   AIO citations).
3. Only top-5 SERP positions produce meaningful AIO overlap; page-2 gives no benefit.
4. Track AIO citations and Google rankings as distinct KPIs — don't use one as a
   proxy for the other.
