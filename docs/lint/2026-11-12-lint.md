## A. Wrong or risky advice (fix first)

These would steer someone to a bad implementation.

1. [[robots-txt-audit-checklist]] has five errors plus duplicates:
   - L52 recommends `robots.txt` + `noindex` together. That contradicts [[robots-txt-strategy]] L212-213 and its own L72: a disallow hides the noindex.
   - L43 says to disallow pagination. That conflicts with [[link-and-anchor-text-best-practices]] L186-189.
   - L46 says to "block JavaScript-rendered URLs" because Googlebot prefers static HTML. That conflicts with [[how-google-search-works]] L25.
   - L102-112 says to block aggressively on new or small sites. That inverts crawl-budget guidance *(outside knowledge)*.
   - L86 says 403s indicate robots.txt denials. They don't.
   - L35, L80 and L82 name retired GSC tools (robots.txt tester, Coverage report).
   - L16/23, L27-29/58-61, L34/93-99 and L35/80 are duplicate items.
2. [[xml-sitemap-optimization-checklist]] L35 says "Remove self-referential canonicals". That contradicts L34 on the same page. It should say "include only URLs whose canonical is self-referential".
3. [[controlling-ai-feature-inclusion]] L25-27 says a robots.txt disallow "removes it from regular Search results". It doesn't; robots.txt controls crawling, not indexing.
4. [[robots-txt-strategy]] has three problems:
   - L224 lists "HTTPS + robots.txt" as an indexing-prevention method. No such mechanism exists.
   - L47 calls `Google-Extended` an "inferred" Gemini user agent. It's a control token ([[llms-txt]] L410), and `GoogleOther` isn't a Gemini agent.
   - L48 and the L97-106 template list only `ClaudeBot`. They're missing Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User, so the template can't allow Claude search while blocking training.
5. Retired crawler names appear in [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] L1942-1945 ("Anthropic-ai") and L310 (Google-Extended as a user agent), and in [[controlling-ai-feature-inclusion]] L63 ("Claude-Web").
6. [[entity-based-seo-implementation]] L217 says ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini "don't directly crawl the web". That contradicts [[robots-txt-strategy]] L39-48.
7. Outdated schema and sitemap details:
   - [[entity-based-seo-implementation]] L166 suggests Freebase as a `sameAs` target. Freebase shut down in 2016.
   - [[brand-entity-seo-strategy]] L89 and L217 use `mention`; the property is `mentions` *(outside knowledge)*.
   - [[image-seo-checklist]] L39-40 recommends `<image:title>` and `<image:caption>`, which Google deprecated in 2022 *(outside knowledge)*.

   - [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] L1888: the GA4 AI-referrer regex includes Neeva and Bard but no claude.ai.
