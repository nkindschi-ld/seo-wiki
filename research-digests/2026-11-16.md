## Studies

### 1. The Popular Get More Popular: Auditing Brand Bias in Generative Search — Politecnico di Milano (Monaco, Lenti, Pierri), 2026-11-05
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07614 · **Type:** Observational audit plus controlled experiment (arXiv preprint, not yet peer-reviewed) · **Rigor:** 5/5
**Method:** Study 1: 80 Italian commercial prompts, 4 sectors, with/without 3 buyer personas, search on/off, 10 repetitions, on gpt-5.5, gemini-3.6-flash and perplexity/sonar (19,200 answers, 1,176 brands, 2026-10-14); mention/citation rates regressed on log Google search volume. Study 2: 24 prompts × 5 real product pages, target page varied by brand and 3 fact-preserving GEO rewrites (38,400 + 9,600 rankings, 2026-10-28 to 11-03). Code and data released. Limits: Italian only, single snapshot, API not consumer UI.
**Key findings:** • Top-popularity-quintile brands are mentioned and cited ~1.4× to 2.8× as often as the average brand • Skew persists with search on and off • Target page ranked 0.44 positions higher under its true brand than an invented one • Fact-preserving GEO rewrites moved rank ~0.02 positions (not significant) • Buyer personas were the only factor weakening the skew
**Conflicts of interest:** none apparent (academic).
**Wiki fit:** Extends [[ai-visibility-correlation-factors]]; possible conflict with [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] on whether content rewrites move recommendations (agrees with [[c-seo-bench-2025]]'s null results).

### 2. 239,266 brands, ranked by authority in Gemini 4 Hydrogen — DEJAN (Dan Petrovic), 2026-11-13
**Link:** https://dejan.ai/blog/gemini-4-hydrogen-brand-authority/ · **Type:** Model-recall probe · **Rigor:** 4/5
**Method:** Gemini 4 Hydrogen via Vertex AI, no search tools; 4-step prompt chain; authority = personalized PageRank over the model's ranked brand lists from 420 seeds; 52,923 calls, 239,266 brands, 4.4M links, run 2026-11-13. Limits stated: one link level, recall not market standing.
**Key findings:** • Apple (100), Microsoft (94.3), Google (90.9) lead; 9 of top 10 are tech, Nike 10th • Windows ranks #11 though never named when brands are asked directly • IKEA ranks #149 • 94% of 17,009 reasoning summaries mention competitors
**Conflicts of interest:** DEJAN sells SEO/AI-search consulting; model recall may not reflect live retrieval.
**Wiki fit:** Extends [[parametric-memory-vs-grounding]] (parametric brand authority); agrees with [[brand-entity-seo-strategy]].

**Conflicts of interest:** Profound sells AI visibility tracking; the study shows visibility is movable.
**Wiki fit:** Extends [[ai-answer-volatility]]; possible conflict with [[ai-citation-landscape]] on content-freshness.

### 4. Characterizing Adversarial GEO via Implicit Brand Injection — Indian Institute of Science (Kriplani, Wang), 2026-11-02
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04005 · **Type:** Controlled benchmark (arXiv preprint, not yet peer-reviewed) · **Rigor:** 4/5
**Method:** Pipeline inserts one non-promotional brand mention into 1,000 articles (12 domains, 60 synthetic brands); bge-base-en-v1.5 retrieval (e5 check) and 5 generators (GPT-5-mini, GPT-OSS-20B, Qwen3-8B, Gemma2-9B, Llama-3.1-8B); 8-annotator study on 50 paragraphs. Period: named model versions. Code released. Limits: synthetic brands, dense retrievers only, LLM judge.
**Key findings:** • Best case brand mention rate 62.7%, propagation 75.9% • Retrieval Hit@10 82.2% in top setting • Beat best-of-N in all 20 conditions • Humans flagged 31% of injected paragraphs as promotional vs 57% for direct prompting
**Conflicts of interest:** none apparent; dual-use attack research.
**Wiki fit:** Extends the adversarial-manipulation note in [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] and [[kumar-lakkaraju-manipulating-llms-2024]].
