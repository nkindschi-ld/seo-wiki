## Studies

### 1. What Drives AI Citations? Content, Retrieval and Model Knowledge in GEO — University of Toronto (Mahe Chen, Nick Koudas), 2026-10-10
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15033 · **Type:** observational panel plus API replay and controlled experiments (preprint, not yet peer-reviewed) · **Rigor:** 5/5
**Method:** 90 days of daily answers from ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity Sonar (2026-06-24 to 2026-09-21); 158,099 answers and 468,037 citations over 706 queries; within-website regression with robustness rules, variance decomposition, API replay of ChatGPT retrieval, no-search probes of model priors; data, logs and code released
**Key findings:** • Within the same website, title match (+14.1% citations per SD) and best-paragraph match (+15.5%) are the robust content effects • FAQ blocks, summary boxes, author markers, schema.org markup and page speed look helpful across sites but aren't significant within them (FAQ +18.1% across vs −3.4% within) • Retrieval explains 32% of ChatGPT's within-site citation variation vs 2.8% for page features, and 97% of ChatGPT's retrieved pages are absent from Google's top 50 • 47% of ChatGPT citations come from sites the model names without searching • Only 14–29% of the cited set is still shared ten weeks later
**Conflicts of interest:** none apparent (academic authors; queries were written for monitoring-platform customers, so may skew commercial)
**Wiki fit:** possible conflict with [[structured-data-and-ai-search]] on schema/FAQ citation lift (adds a within-site causal-leaning design to the open JSON-LD conflict); extends [[parametric-memory-vs-grounding]] and [[ai-answer-volatility]]

### 2. Location, Location, Generation: Where You Ask Matters for GEO — University of Toronto (Mahe Chen, Nick Koudas), 2026-10-16
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24081 · **Type:** controlled audit (preprint, not yet peer-reviewed) · **Rigor:** 5/5
**Method:** 300 questions across 10 commercial topics, varying declared location (19 cities) and network location (5 VPN exits) on ChatGPT and Gemini; every request sent twice for a noise baseline; 31,200 answers, October 2026; Jaccard localization index with bootstrap CIs; data and code released
**Key findings:** • ChatGPT ignores network location but follows declared location; Gemini follows network country, not city • A request from one US city recommends only 8% of the businesses the same request gets from another city (vs 32% for repeats in the same city) • Monitoring one city captures 9% of near-me businesses seen across 15 cities; ten cities capture 69%
**Conflicts of interest:** none apparent
**Wiki fit:** extends [[ai-visibility-measurement-methodology]] (single-location tracking badly undercounts local visibility) and [[personal-context-signals-in-ai-search]]

### 3. Which Metadata Matters for LLM Tool Selection in MCP? — University of Delaware (Xiaofan Li, Fengchun Qiao, Xing Gao), 2026-10-09
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14334 · **Type:** controlled experiment plus ecosystem analysis (preprint, not yet peer-reviewed) · **Rigor:** 5/5
**Method:** 367,174 MCP tool records from five registries; single-factor interventions on server name, tool name, description and parameter schema across 5 LLMs (GPT-5.4 mini, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.5 Flash, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Qwen3.7-Plus) and 5 tasks, positions rotated; conditional logit; scripts and seeds released. No calendar period stated, so the pinned model versions stand in for it
**Key findings:** • Parameter schemas have the largest, most consistent effect on which tool is picked (23 of 28 estimates significant; median magnitude 3.82 vs 1.48 for server names) • Descriptions that explain parameters raise selection; tool names rarely matter (6 of 29 significant) • Renaming parameter `to` to `recipient_id` cut selection to 1–10%, while `receiver` kept 35–51%
**Conflicts of interest:** none apparent
**Wiki fit:** extends [[ai-coding-agent-tool-selection]] and [[docs-over-mcp]] (first controlled evidence on MCP metadata as a selection lever)

### 4. From Public Posts to AI-Search Citations: Measuring the Fragility of AI Search — Fudan University (Qi Liu, Geng Hong, Min Yang et al.), 2026-10-08
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11932 · **Type:** measurement study plus controlled planting experiments (preprint, listed for NDSS 2027) · **Rigor:** 4/5
**Method:** citation mapping across 10 AI search platforms (17,211 citations, 6,356 domains, March 2026); marker-controlled posts on fabricated topics tracked with browser automation (April 2026; 27-article baseline, 30-post replication, 53-post volume test); survey of 13 GEO providers
**Key findings:** • 8 of 10 platforms cited a fabricated concept within seven days of posting • A self-hosted WordPress site got zero citations after 40 posts, while high-preference UGC platforms produced the first signal every round • Citations rose 5.3× as posts went from 1 to 20, and some persisted after the source pages were deleted
**Conflicts of interest:** none apparent (academic security researchers)
**Wiki fit:** extends [[serp-poisoning]] and [[ai-mediated-commercial-persuasion]]; adds non-English engines (Doubao, Yuanbao, Kimi) to [[ai-citation-landscape]]

### 5. How ChatGPT Does Query Fan-Out (15 Million Queries Studied) — Ahrefs (Louise Linehan, Xibeijia Guan), 2026-10-12
**Link:** https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt/ · **Type:** large-scale log analysis · **Rigor:** 4/5
**Method:** 66.6M logged-out GPT-5.6 Luna responses and their 15M fan-out queries (Aug–Sep 2026), plus a 20,000-prompt official-source test, ~1M `site:` queries and Brand Radar citation trends (Mar–Sep 2026); limitations stated
**Key findings:** • 14.4% of responses contained fan-out queries, averaging ~1.6 per response • 6.8% of fan-out queries used `site:`, and 75.9% of the most common `site:` searches targeted brand sites • Reddit citations in ChatGPT fell 88% from March to September (from 1 in 5 answers to 1 in 43)
**Conflicts of interest:** Ahrefs sells Brand Radar AI visibility tracking, the source of the citation data; brand-site findings favor its products
**Wiki fit:** possible conflict with [[query-fanout]] on fan-out frequency and `site:` share (wiki holds 2.1 vs 7.61 sub-queries and a 0.3%→23% `site:` rise); possible conflict with [[ai-citation-landscape]] on Reddit's citation share

### 6. The half-life of an AI citation is 11 days — Profound (Davis McCain), 2026-09-30
**Link:** https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/the-half-life-of-an-ai-citation-is-11-days · **Type:** longitudinal survival analysis · **Rigor:** 4/5
**Method:** deterministic 1-in-128 sample of cited URLs across 7 engines, measured daily 2025-09-08 to 2026-09-08; 883,000 pages and 1,188,816 page-engine lifecycles; Kaplan-Meier with Greenwood 95% intervals; no limitations section
**Key findings:** • The median page loses half its peak citation share within 11 days of peaking; 78% do so within two weeks • Pages cited by 5+ engines are ~5.5× as likely to still hold at day 56 (6.8% vs 1.2%) • Among sustained pages, a Claude citation is 2.8× as likely as a ChatGPT one to hold at eight weeks
**Conflicts of interest:** Profound sells citation tracking; fast decay argues for continuous monitoring
**Wiki fit:** extends the open "how fast cited sources churn" conflict in [[ai-citation-landscape]] and [[ai-answer-volatility]] with a page-level half-life measure

### 7. How Stable Are AI Citations? 10 Things We Learned Tracking 37,500 Citations — Ahrefs (Mateusz Makosiewicz, Xibeijia Guan), 2026-10-29
**Link:** https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-stable-are-ai-citations/ · **Type:** longitudinal daily tracking · **Rigor:** 4/5
**Method:** websites cited on day one across five AI tools, tracked daily for 20 days (~37,500 citations; per-tool question counts given); page-factor comparison, 230-question ranking analysis, 55-brand manual check; validation data 2026-08-28 to 2026-10-04; thorough limitations
**Key findings:** • 50–83% of citations were gone the next day, but ~75% of dropouts returned within 20 days • On AIO and AI Mode, pages ranking in Google for the question were ~7× more likely to stay cited a week or more • 64% of Gemini and 53% of Copilot answers had no citations at all
**Conflicts of interest:** Ahrefs sells SEO tools and Brand Radar; volatility tied to rankings supports both
**Wiki fit:** agrees with [[ai-answer-volatility]]'s stable-core-plus-churning-tail model; feeds the open churn conflict in [[ai-citation-landscape]]

### 8. AI Overviews Cut CTR by 23.1% in France — Ahrefs (Juliette Begue, Xibeijia Guan), 2026-09-16
**Link:** https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-france-impact/ · **Type:** natural experiment with control group · **Rigor:** 4/5
**Method:** aggregated Search Console data for 963 of France's top-1,000 click domains, 28 days before vs 9 days after AIO launched in France (2026-07-22); high-exposure (>20% AIO) vs near-unaffected control (<2%); short post window acknowledged
**Key findings:** • Median CTR for highly exposed domains fell 23.1% in nine days (control-adjusted −24.8%) • 82.2% of highly exposed domains lost CTR • Roughly one CTR point lost per point of AIO exposure
**Conflicts of interest:** Ahrefs sells AI-visibility tools; AIO traffic loss raises demand for them
**Wiki fit:** extends [[aio-ctr-impact]] with a controlled launch-event estimate in a new market

### 13. Which Types of Mentions Correlate the Strongest With AI Visibility? (260M AI Prompts Analyzed) — Ahrefs (Louise Linehan, Xibeijia Guan), 2026-11-03
**Link:** https://ahrefs.com/blog/mention-types-ai-visibility/ · **Type:** correlational analysis · **Rigor:** 3/5
**Method:** Spearman correlation between seven off-site mention types and unbranded AI visibility (AIO, AI Mode, ChatGPT) for 10,000 US brands over 260M prompts, using Brand Radar and Content Explorer, with analysis inside search-volume bands to control for brand size; collection period not stated (current snapshot), so capped at 3
**Key findings:** • Reddit mentions in ranking threads correlate most strongly with AI visibility (0.75); TikTok weakest (0.60) • 10× more Reddit mentions is associated with ~5.5× more AI mentions (webpage mentions: ~3.7×) • Within search-volume bands, correlations drop to 0.34–0.54, so brand size explains part of the link
**Conflicts of interest:** Ahrefs sells Brand Radar and Content Explorer, the tools used to measure both sides

### 14. What Drives Citations in Production LLMs? Two Million AI Citations Across Ten Thousand Pages — Discovered Labs (Ben Moore, Liam Dunne), 2026-09-28
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35077 · **Type:** observational multi-method study (preprint, not yet peer-reviewed) · **Rigor:** 3/5
**Method:** ~2.1M citation records from ChatGPT, Claude, AIO and Gemini across 19 B2B SaaS workspaces over ~6 months (exact dates not given); 10,042 crawled pages with 60+ features; mixed-effects regression with domain fixed effects, stability-selection Lasso, double ML, SHAP, holdouts; data not released; some internal numeric inconsistencies
**Key findings:** • Prompt-content alignment is the dominant page-level predictor (β +0.37) • FAQ sections, author bios and Core Web Vitals are not significant after domain controls • Domain authority carries ~6× the SHAP weight of the strongest non-alignment page feature
**Conflicts of interest:** material and undisclosed: Discovered Labs sells AEO services and the data come from its own platform

### 15. From Prompt to Recommendation: A Fitted Stage Model of Brand Visibility in AI Search — Aiso Boost (Benjamin Tannenbaum), 2026-09-19
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23162 · **Type:** predictive modeling on platform data (preprint, not yet peer-reviewed) · **Rigor:** 3/5
**Method:** 34,960 unbranded GPT and Gemini observations over 2,854 prompts in 75 projects (June–Sept 2026) plus three small cohorts; risk ratios with bootstrap CIs, chronological 70/30 holdout; raw data not released
**Key findings:** • With neither own-domain citation nor branded fan-out, a brand is mentioned in 2.8–3.8% of unbranded answers; with both, 91–100% • Own-domain citation is associated with ~9–11× higher mention risk • A model using prior-run history plus live retrieval signals reaches held-out AUC 0.94–0.96
**Conflicts of interest:** material and disclosed: sole author is CEO of Aiso Boost, which sells AI-search visibility measurement; data from its platform
**Wiki fit:** possible conflict with [[topical-authority-in-ai-search]] on the mentions-vs-citations decoupling (−0.229); extends [[ai-visibility-correlation-factors]]
